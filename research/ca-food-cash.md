# California Low-Income Food and Cash Programs (2026)

**Research date:** 2026-10-11
**Scope:** CalFresh, CFAP, CalWORKs, SSI/SSP, CAPI, General Relief, WIC, universal school meals and SUN Bucks, state tax credits, other CA food programs.
**Reference FPL (2026):** $15,960 for 1 person, +$5,680 per additional person (4 persons = $33,000).
**Method:** WebSearch and WebFetch. Several official CDSS and county pages did not render their numbers, so figures came from secondary sources (LAO, county fact sheets, search snippets). Those are flagged in the text. **UNCONFIRMED** means no source I could read confirmed the item.

**Derived figures (my arithmetic, not from a source):**
- 200% FPL, 1 person: $31,920/yr = $2,660/mo. 4 persons: $66,000/yr = $5,500/mo.
- 100% FPL (net), 1 person: $1,330/mo. 4 persons: $2,750/mo.

## 1. CalFresh (CA SNAP)

- **Who:** Gross income at or below 200% FPL under broad-based categorical eligibility (BBCE). Net income at or below 100% FPL. No asset test under BBCE. SSI recipients are eligible in CA (CDSS "Expanding CalFresh to People with SSI" topic: https://www.cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/calfresh). The 2019 start date is **UNCONFIRMED** (I did not read the page body).
  - 1 person: gross limit reported as **$2,610/mo** for Oct 1, 2025 to Sep 30, 2026 (San Diego County: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/content/sdc/hhsa/programs/ssp/food_stamps/income_limits.html; San Luis Obispo County: https://slocounty.ca.gov/departments/social-services/calfresh). Net limit $1,305/mo (same source).
  - FY 2026-27 limits (effective Oct 1, 2026) were **not found**. Using 2026 FPL, the gross limit should be about $2,660/mo (derived).
  - 4 persons: **UNCONFIRMED** for CalFresh. Derived 200% = $5,500/mo.
  - Immigrant rules: **UNCONFIRMED** from a fetched source. See the CalFresh immigrant narrowing below.
- **Amount:** Varies by household size, countable income and expenses (rent, utilities). Issued monthly on EBT. Source: https://www.cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/calfresh
- **Changes (2025-2026):**
  - **H.R. 1 work rules (ABAWD):** The time limit now covers adults through age 64 (previously 54). It covers adults caring for dependents 14 and older (previously under 18). Exemptions for former foster youth, veterans and homeless people were removed. Most CA counties began applying the rules **June 1, 2026**. Applicants before June 1 generally face the rules at recertification. First discontinuances were expected **October 2026**. Source: https://www.lao.ca.gov/Publications/Report/5126 (search snippet; LAO PDF unreadable). County waivers run through October 2026 for Alpine, Colusa, Imperial, Merced, Monterey, Plumas and Tulare. Source: https://www.fresnocountyca.gov/Departments/Social-Services/Assistance-Programs/CalFresh-Changes-ABAWD-Work-Rules
  - **Immigrant eligibility:** Narrowing took effect **April 2026**. Asylees, refugees, parolees, battered noncitizens and trafficking victims lost federal eligibility. LAO estimates about 72,000 people will lose benefits. Source: https://www.lao.ca.gov/handouts/socservices/2026/Major-H.R.-1-Changes-040826.pdf (search snippet). Stanislaus County notes May 2026 was the first discontinuance month: https://www.stancounty.com/bos/agenda/2026/20260505/DIS02.pdf
  - **Cost shift:** Starting **Oct 2027**, states with a payment error rate of 6% or higher pay a share of benefits. CA's error rate: **UNCONFIRMED**. County admin share projected to rise from $399M to $607M (May Revision projection). Source: LAO search snippet above.
- **How to apply:**
  - Online: BenefitsCal.com. GetCalFresh.org states "You can no longer apply for CalFresh on this website." Source: https://www.getcalfresh.org/
  - Phone: CalFresh Benefits Helpline **1-877-847-3663**. Source: https://www.cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/calfresh
  - In person: your county social services (CalFresh) office.

## 2. California Food Assistance Program (CFAP)

- **Who:** State-funded food aid for income-eligible noncitizens who are not eligible for CalFresh because of immigration status. About 50,000 people (LAO). Age and status rules follow the current program design; the state-funded expansion below has not started.
- **Amount:** Equivalent to CalFresh benefits (**UNCONFIRMED** for the exact schedule).
- **Changes:**
  - **Expansion to undocumented adults 55+:** Originally planned for 2025-26. The 2024-25 budget delayed it by two years. Benefits are now expected to begin **October 1, 2027** (2027-28), subject to appropriation. Source: https://www.lao.ca.gov/Publications/Report/5086/3 (search snippet). Advocates cite about 90,000 to 130,000 eligible residents (LAO Jan 2025 estimate). Sources: https://nourishca.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/3.3-2025-Budget-Sign-on-Food4All.pdf
  - **2026-27 budget:** Advocates report the final deal keeps the 55+ expansion (Oct 2027 start) and automation funds (~$38M GF reappropriated). Advocates report it dropped a $5M proposal to cover humanitarian immigrants who lost CalFresh under H.R. 1. Source (advocacy, one-sided): https://nourishca.org/whats-fresh/news-and-media-releases/press-release/food4all-2026-27-state-budget/ . Official enactment date and final text: **UNCONFIRMED**.
  - **Backfill for H.R. 1 cuts:** No state backfill was confirmed. Advocates estimate about 72,000 humanitarian immigrants could lose food assistance by early 2027 without state action. LAO projects combined CalFresh and CFAP benefits fall $833M in 2026-27, mostly from enrollment drops. Source: LAO search snippet (https://www.lao.ca.gov/Publications/Report/5208/3 ).
- **How to apply:** Through your county social services office, same as CalFresh (**UNCONFIRMED** for the CFAP-specific process; the county office route is standard).

## 3. CalWORKs (CA TANF)

- **Who:** Families with children whose income is below the MBSAC-based income limits, which vary by assistance unit size and region (Region 1 or Region 2). Source: https://www.cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/calworks-summary/program-overview/about-calworks
  - Earned income limits: **UNCONFIRMED** (the CDSS chart did not render).
- **Maximum Aid Payment (MAP), family of 3, no other income:**
  - Non-exempt, Region 1: **$1,175/mo** (effective Oct 1, 2024; San Francisco 2026 fact sheet: https://www.sfhsa.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-06/factsheet_calworks_english_6.30.26.pdf ). Derived from the Oct 2023 rate ($1,171) plus 0.3%.
  - Exempt, Region 1: **$1,310/mo** on the Oct 2023 CDSS table (https://www.cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/calworks-summary/program-overview/about-calworks). LA County cites $1,314 (Oct 2024). Use $1,314 as current (derived).
  - Non-exempt, Region 2 (Oct 2023): $1,112. Derived 2024 value ~$1,115. **UNCONFIRMED** for 2026.
  - Exempt, Region 2 (Oct 2023): $1,244. Derived 2024 value ~$1,248. **UNCONFIRMED** for 2026.
  - Whether an Oct 2025 MAP increase occurred: **UNCONFIRMED**. The most recent confirmed change was 0.3% on Oct 1, 2024, funded from the Child Poverty and Family Supplemental Support Subaccount.
- **Time limit:** **60 months** for adults, not 48. AB 79 raised it from 48 to 60 effective **May 1, 2022**. Months with a welfare-to-work exemption don't count. Notices go out at months 54 and 57. Sources: https://stgenssa.sccgov.org/debs/program_handbooks/calworks/assets/39Time_Limits/CalWORKs_60Month_Lifetime_Limit.htm
- **Child care:** Stage 1 (county-managed, starts when a parent enters CalWORKs). Stage 2 (when employment is stable or on transition off aid). Stage 3 (when funded space is available). Source: same as above. Dollar subsidy rates: **UNCONFIRMED**.
- **Housing Support Program / homeless assistance:** Details **UNCONFIRMED** (not in sources I read). Check your county welfare department CalWORKs page.
- **Changes:** Income reporting threshold for FY 2026 effective Oct 1, 2025 (LSNC summary, reporting threshold only). Source: https://wordpress.lsnc.net/page/7/
- **How to apply:** Your county welfare department (Department of Social Services or Human Services). Also via BenefitsCal.com. **UNCONFIRMED** phone number for each county.

## 4. SSI/SSP (Aged, Blind, Disabled)

- **Who:** Aged 65+, blind or disabled, low income and resources. Federal SSI plus the CA State Supplementary Payment (SSP).
- **Amounts (monthly, living independently):**
  - **Individual 2026: $1,233.94** ($994.00 SSI + $239.94 SSP)
  - **Couple 2026: $2,098.83** ($1,491.00 SSI + $607.83 SSP)
  - 2027 projection: individual $1,263.94; couple $2,144.83 (federal 3.1% COLA estimate; state SSP unchanged).
  - Source: https://www.lao.ca.gov/Publications/Report/5148
- **Changes:** No SSP increase in 2025-26 or 2026-27. The last SSP increase was 9.2% in 2023-24. The Governor's budget makes a technical GF reduction for an expected federal overcharge reimbursement that has not materialized (LAO). Source: same as above.
- **How to apply:** Social Security Administration at **ssa.gov/ssi** or **1-800-772-1213**. SSI/SSP-related CA questions: county office.

## 5. CAPI (Cash Assistance Program for Immigrants)

- **Who:** State-funded cash aid for aged (65+), blind or disabled noncitizens who are ineligible for SSI only because of immigration status. Must live in CA and meet income and resource limits. Applicants must have applied for SSI within the past six months and have proof of denial based on immigration status. Source: https://santacruzhumanservices.org/Portals/0/ebsd/Fact%20Sheets/CAPI-Fact-Sheet%201.2026.pdf
- **Amount (2026, Santa Cruz County fact sheet):** Aged or disabled individual, independent living: **$1,233.94/mo**. Blind individual: $1,318.32. Aged or disabled couple: **$2,098.83**. Same as SSI/SSP. Source: same.
- **Changes:** Figures track the 2026 SSI/SSP increase. Older sources cite lower amounts (db101 $954.72 individual).
- **How to apply:** County social services office. State form SOC 814 (**UNCONFIRMED** for the current version). Source: search snippet only.

## 6. General Assistance / General Relief (county-run)

- **LA County General Relief (GR):**
  - Maximum monthly grant: **$221 for 1 person**, **$375 for 2 persons applying together**. Up to 9 months within any 12-month period for employable participants in START. Source: https://dpss.lacounty.gov/content/dam/dpss/documents/en/gr/gr-redesign/powerpoint-presentations/2025-gr-overview-ppt.pdf (2025 presentation).
  - Proposals to raise the grant to $342 (Dec 2024) and $500 (Mar 2025) exist. Adoption: **UNCONFIRMED**.
  - Apply: DPSS General Relief office (see https://dpss.lacounty.gov/). Phone: **UNCONFIRMED**.
- **San Diego County:** Grant amount **UNCONFIRMED**. Income limits listed as $598 (single) and $819 (married couple), undated. Source: https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/hhsa/programs/ssp/general_relief/eligibility_requirements.html
- **Changes:** Varies by county. Some counties have time limits (for example, 33 counties have three-month limits for START participants; 13 have none).

## 7. WIC (Women, Infants, and Children)

- **Who:** Pregnant or postpartum people, infants and children under 5, at or below 185% FPL, or on Medi-Cal, CalFresh or CalWORKs (automatic qualification).
  - 1 person: **$2,461/mo** (185% FPL, USDA table effective July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027; annual $29,526). Source: https://fns-prod.azureedge.us/wic/fr-042926 (search snippet).
  - 4 persons: **$61,050/yr** (about $5,088/mo) per the same USDA table as cited in the search snippet (not fetched directly).
- **Benefit amounts:** Food package (**UNCONFIRMED** dollar values).
- **Changes:** New federal table effective July 1, 2026 (cited above). Some third-party sites show older figures ($2,413 for 1 person).
- **How to apply:** Local WIC agency. Find one at https://www.cdph.ca.gov/Programs/CFH/DWICSN/Pages/default.aspx (CDPH WIC). Phone **UNCONFIRMED**. Documents: pay stub or statement from the last 30 days (30-day grace period). Source: search snippet.

## 8. Universal School Meals and SUN Bucks (Summer EBT)

- **Universal school meals:**
  - Free breakfast and lunch for all public school students who request them. Required since SY 2022-23 under **Education Code 49501.5**. Source: https://www.cde.ca.gov/ls/nu/univmealsfaq.asp
  - Enacted in the budget (AB 130 per local news). Funding is subject to annual appropriation (2024-25: $1.846B Prop 98 GF). Source: https://www.cde.ca.gov/LS/NU/statemealmandate.asp
  - 2026-27: Free meals continue; families do not need to apply. Management Bulletin SNP-05-2026 updated state meal pattern requirements.
  - "AB 1957": **UNCONFIRMED** (not found in search).
- **SUN Bucks (CA Summer EBT):**
  - Amount: **$40/month for June, July and August = $120 total per eligible child** (2026). Source: https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/05/21/california-launches-sun-bucks-for-summer-2026-to-help-fight-childhood-food-insecurity/
  - Eligibility: Children who get free or reduced-price school meals, or whose families are in CalFresh, CalWORKs or Medi-Cal. Age range sources conflict (CDSS FAQ says 0-22; Turlock USD says 6-18). Source: https://www.cdss.ca.gov/sun-bucks/faq
  - **2026 application deadline was August 31, 2026 (passed).** Auto-enrollment covers 3.5M+ kids. Late applicants: **UNCONFIRMED** whether a 2026 window remains.
  - Changes: First CA SUN Bucks year was 2024 (**UNCONFIRMED** for the amount in prior years).
  - Help line: **(877) 328-9677** (search snippet, CDSS-linked).
  - Apply: Through child's school (school meal application or Universal Benefits Application) or via CalFresh/CalWORKs/Medi-Cal enrollment. Source: CDSS FAQ link above.

## 9. State Tax Credits (Tax Year 2025)

- **CalEITC:** 2025 maximum and income ceiling **UNCONFIRMED**. The FTB eligibility page shows a $30,000 limit and maximums of $1,843 (one child), $3,037 (two), $3,417 (three or more). These appear to be older-year figures (the 2024 maximum for three or more was $3,644 per the FTB report). Source: https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/personal/credits/caleitc/eligibility-and-credit-information.html (page does not match 2025; check the FTB 3514 instructions).
- **Young Child Tax Credit (YCTC), 2025:**
  - Max **$1,189** per return.
  - Earned income up to **$32,900**.
  - Child under 6 at end of 2025 and CalEITC eligibility required.
  - Source: https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/personal/credits/young-child-tax-credit.html (search snippet cites the FTB figures).
- **Foster Youth Tax Credit (FYTC):** Additional **$1,083** (indexed annually from 2023) for foster youth who qualify for CalEITC and meet other requirements. Source: FTB 2023 CalEITC/YCTC report (https://www.ftb.ca.gov/about-ftb/data-reports-plans/California-Earned-Income-Tax-Credit-and-Young-Child-Tax-credit-Report-2023.pdf ). 2025 indexed amount **UNCONFIRMED**.
- **Free filing:**
  - CalFile (FTB's free filing tool): https://www.ftb.ca.gov (**UNCONFIRMED** for the exact URL).
  - VITA: free help for low-income filers (**UNCONFIRMED**, not fetched).

## 10. Other California Food Programs

- **CalFresh Fruit & Vegetable EBT pilot:**
  - Status: **Paused July 1, 2026** because incentive funds ran out. Earned incentives on EBT cards can still be used until they expire. A 2026-27 funding fix of about $20M was reported but **UNCONFIRMED** as enacted. Source: https://hoodline.com/2026/07/sacramento-scrambles-to-revive-calfresh-veggie-perk-with-20-million-lifeline/
  - Current law sunsets the pilot on **Jan 1, 2027**. AB 936 (extension) failed. Source: https://www.govbuddy.com/california/bills/ab-936/
  - 91 grocery stores and one farmers' market served about 67,000 households per month (Feb 2026 coalition letter).
- **CalFresh Restaurant Meals Program (RMP):**
  - Active per 2026 county materials. Eligible: homeless, disabled and/or 60+ CalFresh recipients (all household members must qualify). State lists 15 participating counties (page undated). Source: https://www.ebtproject.ca.gov/Clients/calfreshrmp.html
  - Sacramento and LA County note the June 1, 2026 work-rule changes; exempt recipients keep access.
  - How to apply: via your county CalFresh office.
- **Senior food (CSFP / Brown Bag):**
  - CSFP is a USDA program run by CDSS. Ages 60+ with income at or below a threshold.
  - Threshold conflicts: 150% FPL (LA Regional Food Bank; 1 person $1,995/mo for 2026: https://www.lafoodbank.org/programs/commodity-supplemental-food-program/ ) vs 130% FPL (FIND Food Bank page). Official CDSS threshold: **UNCONFIRMED**.
  - Apply: local food bank or CDSS CSFP page. **UNCONFIRMED** phone.
- **Food bank finder:** The California Association of Food Banks finder is the expected source (**UNCONFIRMED** URL, not fetched). Local example: https://www.foodbankccs.org/find-food/calfresh/
