# California Housing, Utility, Child Care, Education and Legal Programs (2026)

**Research date:** 2026-10-11
**Scope:** California-specific housing, utility, child care, education and legal/navigation programs, current as of 2026. Facts were gathered with WebSearch and WebFetch. Official pages were preferred, but several official sites returned certificate or 403 errors, so some facts rely on search snippets or secondary sources. Those are flagged.
**Reference FPL (provided):** 2026 federal poverty line = $15,960 for 1 person, +$5,680 per additional person.

**Legend:** **UNCONFIRMED** = could not confirm from a source in this session. Source URLs are inline.

## HOUSING

### 1. Section 8 / Public Housing (Housing Choice Voucher) in California

- **Who:** Households below the very low income limit (50% AMI). Preferences and local rules vary by housing authority.
- **Amount:** Rent subsidy, generally 30% of adjusted income toward rent (standard HUD rule). UNCONFIRMED for CA-specific figures.
- **Waitlist status (as of Sept 2026, aggregator data; verify with each agency):**
  - **HACLA (Housing Authority of the City of Los Angeles)** (hacla.org): Section 8 list last opened Oct 17–30, 2022 (random lottery, ~30,000 placed). Aggregator listings say closed as of Sept 2026; no reopening date announced. Source: https://lakebalboanc.org/?p=10647 (lottery reference), https://affordablehousingonline.com/open-section-8-waiting-lists/California/Los-Angeles (conflicting listing, not authoritative). **Treat as closed; confirm with HACLA.**
  - **San Diego (SDHC / County Housing Authority):** Conflicting aggregator data. Most say closed; one says last opened Feb 2026 and remains closed. Source: https://affordablehousingonline.com/open-section-8-waiting-lists/California/San-Diego
  - **Oakland Housing Authority:** Aggregator says closed, waitlist over 1,500. Source: https://affordablehousingonline.com/open-section-8-waiting-lists/California/Oakland
  - **Sacramento Housing & Redevelopment Agency (SHRA):** Conflicting (open for preference/emergency only, or closed as of July 2026). Average wait ~35 months per aggregator. Source: https://affordablehousingonline.com/open-section-8-waiting-lists/California/Sacramento
  - **LACDA (LA County Development Authority), SF Housing Authority, Orange County Housing Authority, Santa Clara (Housing Authority of the County of Santa Clara):** **UNCONFIRMED** (no status found in this session).
- **2026 HUD very low income limit (50% AMI), 1 person:**
  - Los Angeles County: **$58,300** (2025 was $53,000). Source: https://planning.lacounty.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Income-Limits-2026.pdf (also cited in https://www.burbankca.gov/documents/173607/0/2026+AFFORDABLE+RENTS+AND+INCOMES+CHART.pdf). Effective date reported as 6/23/2026.
  - San Francisco, San Diego, Sacramento: **UNCONFIRMED.** The HCD 2026 income-limits PDF (https://www.hcd.ca.gov/sites/default/files/docs/grants-and-funding/income-limits-2026.pdf) could not be parsed.
- **Changes 2025–26:** HUD's updated limits apply to certain HUD-assisted properties from May 1, 2026, for new move-ins only. Source: https://leadingage.org/hud-posts-delayed-2026-income-limits-updates-fair-market-rents/
- **How to apply:** Contact each housing authority directly (websites: hacla.org, lacda.org, sdhc.org, sfha.org, oakha.org, shra.org; **UNCONFIRMED from memory**, not verified in this session). Check the aggregator at https://affordablehousingonline.com for waitlist alerts.

### 3. Homeless and Emergency Housing

- **CalWORKs Homeless Assistance (HA):**
  - *Who:* Families eligible or apparently eligible for CalWORKs who are homeless or at risk. Must not have received the same HA within the last 12 months. Source: https://ha.saccounty.gov/content/dam/ha/docs/calworks/CalWORKs%20Fact%20Sheet%20July%202026.pdf
  - *Amount:* Temporary shelter: $85/day for up to 4 members, +$15/day per additional member, up to $145/day. Permanent: average ~$1,644 for deposits, rent or arrears (one listing; **UNCONFIRMED** as current). Source: https://ahum.assembly.ca.gov/media/1378
  - *2026 change:* CalWORKs resource limit rises to **$12,552** ($18,829 if a member is over 60 or disabled), effective Jan 1, 2026. Source: same Sacramento fact sheet.
  - *How to apply:* Through your county human services agency or BenefitsCal.com. **UNCONFIRMED** phone numbers.
- **Coordinated Entry (LA County / LAHSA):**
  - *Families:* Call **211 LA County** (2-1-1). A caller with a minor child under 17 or who is pregnant, and who is homeless or at risk within 14 days, is pre-screened and referred to one of eight Family Solutions Centers. Source: https://211la.org/coordinated-entry-system-families-cesf
  - *Adults/youth:* Access centers, outreach teams, or 2-1-1. Source: https://lahsa.org/ces/home/about
  - *Phone:* LAHSA Information Line (213) 225-6581 and (800) 548-6047 for CES lead agencies, both from older county/provider sheets. **UNCONFIRMED as current.**
- **Stay Housed LA (LA County eviction defense):**
  - *What:* Free eviction defense and legal help through nearly 30 nonprofit law firms and community orgs. In 2025: 3,108 tenants represented in unlawful detainer cases; 10,785 received limited services.
  - *2026 status:* Funding uncertain. City Council approved contracts in March 2026; a July 2, 2026 City Attorney update said $177M in contracts were being finalized. **Execution not confirmed.**
  - *How to apply:* StayHousedLA.org or **(888) 694-0040** (per LAFLA release). Sources: https://lafla.org/stories-events/a-new-partnership-to-prevent-evictions/ and https://cityattorney.lacity.gov/updates/city-attorney-hydee-feldstein-soto-files-second-public-report-re-stay-housed-la
- **Renter protections (state):**
  - *AB 1482 (Tenant Protection Act):* Annual rent increase cap = 5% + local CPI, max **10%**. Current increase limits vary by city and CPI. San Francisco's AB 1482 max for Aug 1, 2026–Jul 31, 2027 is **8.8%** (prior year 6.3%). Source: https://www.sf.gov/reports/august-2024/california-tenant-protection-act-2019-ab-1482. Local rules (e.g., LA RSO units) can be stricter; **UNCONFIRMED** LA figure.
  - *Just cause (Civil Code 1946.2):* Applies after 12 months of tenancy. Sunsets Jan 1, 2030 unless extended. Source: https://bbklaw.com/resources/tenant-protection-act-sets-statewide-rent-caps-and
- **Homekey / Homekey+ (HCD):**
  - Homekey converts hotels/motels to housing for people experiencing homelessness. Homekey+ (Prop 1 bonds) is the successor. Over $2B allocated to Homekey+ for PSH projects (LA County motion, May 5, 2026). Source: https://file.lacounty.gov/SDSInter/bos/supdocs/215891.pdf
  - HCD announced 8 more Homekey+ communities (443 homes) on March 2, 2026. Source: HCD newsroom, https://hcd.ca.gov/about-hcd/newsroom-all
  - Round 3 NOFA: state General Fund portion had to be spent by June 30, 2026. Source: https://www.hcd.ca.gov/sites/default/files/docs/grants-and-funding/homekey/Homekey-Round-3-Notice-of-Funding-Availability.pdf
  - *Who:* Not applied for by individuals. Apply through local jurisdictions or housing developers. Eligibility is set locally.

## UTILITIES

### 4. CARE / FERA / Medical Baseline

- **CARE (California Alternate Rates for Energy):**
  - *Who:* Household income at or below **$43,280/yr for 1–2 persons** (June 1, 2026–May 31, 2027). Adds **$11,360** per additional person (3 people $54,640; 4 $66,000; 5 $77,360). Or any household member on Medi-Cal, CalFresh/SNAP, SSI, or WIC. Note the 1-person limit equals the 2-person limit in the CPUC table. Source: https://webproda.cpuc.ca.gov/industries-and-topics/electrical-energy/electric-costs/care-fera-program (fetch failed; figures from SCE and PG&E pages and search snippets).
  - *Discount:* **SCE: 32.5% off electric** (plus a discounted base services charge of about $6/month). Source: https://www.sce.com/residential/assistance/care-fera. **PG&E, SDG&E, SoCalGas: UNCONFIRMED** from official pages.
  - *2025–26 change:* Limit rose ~$980 for 1–2 persons (from $42,300 to $43,280). Source: https://california.libertyutilities.com/uploads/CalPeco%20ALs/Liberty%20AL%20296-E%20CARE-ESA%202026-27%20Income%20Guidelines.pdf
  - *How to apply:*
    - **SCE:** sce.com (Apply Now); **1-800-798-5723** (SCE page; a second number 800-447-6620 appeared in search results, conflicting).
    - **PG&E:** pge.com/care; **1-866-743-2273** (per PG&E application snippet; **UNCONFIRMED** on the official page). Application PDF: https://www.pge.com/assets/pge/localized/en/docs/account/billing-and-assistance/care-fera-application.pdf
    - **SDG&E / SoCalGas:** **UNCONFIRMED** (not searched).
  - *Recertification:* Required every 2 years (PG&E form). Source: same PG&E application.
- **FERA (Family Electric Rate Assistance):**
  - *Who:* Households between CARE and **250% FPL**. For 2026–27: **$43,281–$54,100 (1–2 persons)**; 3 people $54,641–$68,300; 4 people $66,001–$82,500. Source: SCE page above.
  - *Discount:* **18%** off electric. Source: SCE page above.
- **Medical Baseline (PG&E, SCE, SDG&E, SoCalGas):**
  - *What:* Lower-rate baseline allowance for customers who use life-support or other medical equipment, or who have certain medical conditions. No income limit (per a directory source). Requires doctor certification. Source: PG&E description https://www.pge.com/medicalbaseline (**UNCONFIRMED** page fetch); SCE application https://www.sce.com/residential/assistance/medical-baseline
  - *Discount amount:* **UNCONFIRMED**
  - *How to apply:* Through your utility's website, or the SCE medical baseline application above.

### 6. California LifeLine (phone)

- **What:** State discount on phone service in addition to the federal Lifeline.
- **Amount:** Up to **$19/month** state discount (plus up to $39 for connection/conversion). Combined with federal $9.25, up to **$28.25/month** total. Source: https://www.cpuc.ca.gov/-/media/cpuc-website/about-cpuc/documents/transparency-and-reporting/fact_sheets/california-lifeline/lifeline_onesheet_051126.pdf (search snippet; page not fetched; **verify**).
- **Who:** Income-based (2025–26 table cited: $24,200 for 1 person, $32,600 for 2, +$8,500 per additional) or enrollment in Medi-Cal, SSI, LIHEAP, CalFresh, etc. Source: https://www.cpuc.ca.gov/LifeLine (search snippet). **2026–27 limits UNCONFIRMED.**
- **2026 change:** Since Feb 1, 2026, state and federal LifeLine require separate enrollments; existing customers must renew each separately. Source: https://webproda.cpuc.ca.gov/-/media/cpuc-website/divisions/news-and-outreach/images/featured-stories/california-lifeline-vs-federal-lifeline-021726.pdf
- **How to apply:** californialifeline.com (returned HTTP 403 to fetch). Phone: **UNCONFIRMED**.

### 7. Water bill help and internet

- **LIHWAP (federal water bill program):** **Ended in California.** Federal sunset March 31, 2024. CSD made final "supplemental benefit" payments in May 2024. Source: https://www.csd.ca.gov/Pages/waterbill-faq.aspx. No 2026 restart found. For water help, check local utilities/hardship funds and LIHEAP.
- **California LifeLine Home Broadband Pilot:**
  - *Amount:* $20/month for broadband-only; $30/month for bundled broadband + voice. Up to $39/yr for connection fees. Source: https://broadbandforall.cdt.ca.gov/ca-lifeline-home-broadband-pilot-program/
  - *Who:* Income at or below 135% FPG, or participation in Medi-Cal, SNAP, or SSI. Source: https://broadbandbreakfast.com/with-acp-gone-california-turns-to-lifeline-for-broadband-relief/ (secondary).
  - *2026 change:* Pilot approved by CPUC (Aug 2025; Jan 2026 start per some sources; **conflicting**). The federal ACP ended June 1, 2024.
  - *Provider list (e.g., AT&T, Spectrum, Xfinity):* **UNCONFIRMED** on CPUC page.
  - *How to apply:* Through participating providers, or CPUC info at cpuc.ca.gov. **UNCONFIRMED** phone numbers.

## CHILD CARE AND EDUCATION

### 8. Subsidized Child Care, State Preschool, Universal TK

- **CalWORKs child care (Stages 1–3):**
  - *Stage 3 income threshold:* 70%–85% of area median income, adjusted for family size, effective Jan 1, 2026. Source: https://wordpress.lsnc.net/?p=9538 (secondary; Stage-by-stage details **UNCONFIRMED** from official CDSS).
  - *Stage 2:* Recipients of CalWORKs cash aid in an approved activity or employed are categorically eligible. Source: LSNC summary above.
  - *Family fees (state Family Fee Schedule effective July 1, 2025):* Exempt if: CPS-involved children (up to 12 months), CalWORKs, children with special needs, homeless families in approved counties, or income below 75% SMI. At 75% SMI and above, the fee is capped at 1% of monthly income. Source: https://www.sfhsa.org/sites/default/files/media/document/2026-06/factsheet_calworks_english_6.30.26.pdf and https://santacruzhumanservices.org/Portals/0/ebsd/CW-2.0/CalWORKs-Fact-Sheet%2007.01.2026.pdf
  - *Income ceilings:* 85% SMI (2025–26) for a family of 4: **$108,237** (secondary; 2026–27 **UNCONFIRMED**).
  - *How to apply:* Through your county CalWORKs office (BenefitsCal.com) or a local Alternative Payment / Resource & Referral agency (CCR&R). Names/phone numbers **UNCONFIRMED**. Use 211 to find a local agency.
- **California State Preschool Program (CSPP):**
  - *Who:* Effective **July 1, 2026**, income-eligible if adjusted monthly income is at or below **115% of state median income**, adjusted for family size. Source: CDE Management Bulletin 26-03, https://www.cde.ca.gov/Sp/cd/ci/mb2603.asp
  - *Fees:* Family Fee Schedule FY 2026–27 is in CDE Management Bulletin 26-04 (contents **UNCONFIRMED**). Source: https://www.cde.ca.gov/Sp/cd/ci/mb2603.asp (linked).
  - *Conflict:* One blog says free up to 100% SMI (older). CDE figure is used here.
  - *How to apply:* Contact your local CSPP contractor (school district or county office). Use cde.ca.gov/sp/cd.
- **Universal Transitional Kindergarten (TK):**
  - *Who:* Fully available since 2025–26. For 2026–27, a child who turns 4 by **Sept 1, 2026** qualifies (one Montecito district says Sept 2; **conflict**). Sources: https://www.smfcsd.net/district-departments/student-services/enrollment/universal-tk-expansion and https://www.psusd.us/Page/7911
  - *Cost:* Free (public school).
  - *How to apply:* Your local school district's enrollment office. Kindergarten (turn 5 by Sept 1, 2026): https://www.losal.org/enrollment/kindergarten-enrollment

### 9. College Aid

- **Cal Grant (2026–27 awards, CSAC/LAO):**
  - *Tuition award (max, new full-time):* UC **$15,588**; nonprofit **$9,358**; WASC for-profit **$8,056**; CSU **$6,838**; other for-profit **$4,000**. Cal Grant C private: **$2,462**.
  - *Access award (non-tuition):* Cal Grant A/B **$1,648** (Cal Grant B other students); **$6,000** for students with dependent children or foster youth. Cal Grant C CCC **$1,094**; private **$547**.
  - *Pending:* AB 402 would change tuition awards (not law as of search). Source: https://lao.ca.gov/reports/2026/5127/2026-27_California_Student_Aid_Commission_021726.pdf and https://lao.ca.gov/Education/EdBudget/Details/1122
  - *Deadlines:* 2026–27 priority deadline **March 2, 2026 (passed)**. Final deadline June 30, 2027 (CSULB; **not verified** with CSAC). **2027–28 cycle opens Oct 1, 2026** (UC calendar). Source: https://admission.universityofcalifornia.edu/how-to-apply/applying-as-a-first-year/dates-and-deadlines.html
  - *How to apply:* FAFSA (studentaid.gov) or CA Dream Act Application (dream.csac.ca.gov); GPA Verification Form for first-time Cal Grant. Check dream.csac.ca.gov (CSAC).
- **California College Promise Grant (CCPG, community college fee waiver):**
  - *What:* Waives enrollment fees. Does not cover health, activity, lab/materials, or most parking fees.
  - *Who:* California resident (1 year), or AB 540 / AB 1899 student, or T or U visa holder, or CA-resident homeless youth. Income thresholds (Award B only): one college lists $23,475 for a family of 1 (2026–27 base year), +$8,250 per member. **UNCONFIRMED from official CCC source.** Source: https://www.craftonhills.edu/current-students/financial-aid/bog-fee-waiver (secondary).
  - *2026 change:* **AB 1920** (Chapter 32, Statutes of 2026, signed June 29, 2026) protects fee-waiver eligibility for students called to active duty. Source: https://legiscan.com/CA/text/AB1920/id/3451702
  - *Deadline:* 2026–27 applications through summer 2027 (per one college). Students reapply every year.
  - *How to apply:* icanaffordcollege.com or your community college financial aid office; FAFSA or CADAA. (icanaffordcollege.com not fetched.)
- **Middle Class Scholarship (MCS):**
  - *Who (2026–27 income ceilings):* Dependent students $250,000 household income; independent single without dependents $144,000; married $165,000. Asset ceiling: $250,000 (dependent); $119,000 (independent, one source lists this for married too; **conflict**). Source: https://www.csuchico.edu/fa/_assets/documents/midclassschol27a.pdf and https://www.calstate.edu/apply/paying-for-college/financial-aid/types/Pages/middle-class-scholarship.aspx
  - *Amount:* Not fixed; determined after other aid (23% coverage per Budget Act). Source: LAO snippet.
  - *Deadline:* March 2 (2026–27 deadline passed).
  - *How to apply:* FAFSA or CADAA.
- **California Dream Act (undocumented / AB 540 students):**
  - *How:* File the **California Dream Act Application (CADAA)** at dream.csac.ca.gov. Qualifies for Cal Grant, MCS, and CCPG under state rules. Federal aid not available. Source: https://www.smuhsd.org/families/academic-resources/graduation-requirements/california-dream-act-application-cadaa (secondary; CSAC site **UNCONFIRMED**).

### 11. Navigation: 211 and BenefitsCal

- **211 LA County:** Free, 24/7, multilingual. Dial **2-1-1** or visit https://211la.org. Covers housing, utility, eviction, and LifeLine screening (per Harbor-UCLA reference, dated). Source: https://211la.org/ and https://211la.org/sites/default/files/2025-10/211-la-info-sheet.pdf
- **211ca.org (statewide):** Domain **not confirmed** in this session. 2-1-1 works statewide, but the search returned only 211 LA.
- **BenefitsCal.com:** Statewide portal for CalFresh, Medi-Cal, CalWORKs, General Assistance, and others. Apply, report changes, upload documents, and check status. Also accepts phone, mail, fax, or in-person applications through county offices. Source: https://hhs.marincounty.gov/services/apply-public-assistance-benefits-medi-cal-calfresh-calworks (search snippet, Marin County).
  - **2026 changes noted in search:** Some lawfully present noncitizens lose CalFresh eligibility from April 1, 2026. CalFresh work rules for ABAWD (ages 18–64, no child under 14) effective June 1, 2026 per one county, but LA County says a waiver ended Nov 2, 2025 and federal guidance is pending (**conflict**). Medi-Cal asset limits return ($130,000 one person / $195,000 couple, seniors and disabled). Medi-Cal immigrant changes Oct 2026; community engagement requirement Jan 2027. Source: https://main.sbcounty.gov/2026/04/01/what-to-know-about-calfresh-and-medi-cal-eligibility-updates/
