# Where to apply: every link

Checked 2026-10-11 by loading each URL. ✓ = loaded. Sites that block automated checks (ssa.gov, lawhelpca.org and others) are marked; they are real sites but couldn't be machine-verified.

## Food

### CalFresh / SNAP (food stamps)
- **California:** Your county social services office, or online
  - [benefitscal.com](https://benefitscal.com) ✓
  - Phone: 1-877-847-3663 (CalFresh info line)
- **Other states:** Your state SNAP agency
  - [USDA SNAP state directory](https://www.fns.usda.gov/snap/state-directory) ✓
- **Bring:** Photo ID, Social Security numbers, pay stubs from the last 30 days, rent and utility bills
- **How long:** Up to 30 days. 3 days in California (7 elsewhere) if you have almost no money
- **Tip:** You can turn in the form with just your name, address and signature to lock in your start date, then send documents. Expect a phone interview.

### WIC
- **California:** Your local WIC office (appointment)
  - [myfamily.wic.ca.gov](https://myfamily.wic.ca.gov) ✓
- **Other states:** Your state or local WIC agency
  - [USDA WIC](https://www.fns.usda.gov/wic) ✓
- **Bring:** ID, proof of address, proof of income (a Medi-Cal, CalFresh or SNAP card counts), your child's ID and shot records
- **How long:** Usually approved at your first appointment

### School meals
- **California:** Nothing to apply for. Every public school student eats free. If the school sends a family income form, fill it out, since it can qualify your kids for SUN Bucks.
- **Other states:** The meal application from your school district. Kids in SNAP or TANF households are enrolled automatically.
- **Bring:** Household size and income (on the form)
- **How long:** Usually within days

### SUN Bucks / Summer EBT ($120 per child)
- **California:** Most kids get it automatically through CalFresh, CalWORKs, Medi-Cal or their school. Otherwise apply through CDSS.
  - [cdss.ca.gov](https://www.cdss.ca.gov) ✓
- **Other states:** Your state's Summer EBT program (not every state takes part)
  - [USDA SUN Bucks](https://www.fns.usda.gov/summer/sunbucks) ✓
- **Bring:** Your child's name, school and birth date
- **How long:** Cards arrive by summer. The 2026 application deadline (Aug 31) has passed.

### CFAP (state food aid for immigrants) (California only)
- **California:** Same as CalFresh: your county office. The county checks CFAP for you if federal CalFresh says no.
  - [benefitscal.com](https://benefitscal.com) ✓
- **Bring:** The same papers as CalFresh, plus your immigration documents
- **How long:** Same as CalFresh (up to 30 days)

### Food banks and the senior food box (CSFP)
- **California:** Your local food bank or pantry. Call or text 211 to find one.
  - [Feeding America food bank finder](https://www.feedingamerica.org/find-your-local-foodbank) ✓
  - Phone: 211
- **Other states:** Your local food bank
  - [Feeding America food bank finder](https://www.feedingamerica.org/find-your-local-foodbank) ✓
  - Phone: 211
- **Bring:** Usually nothing. The senior food box (60+) needs ID with your age and proof of income.
- **How long:** Same day at most pantries

## Cash

### CalWORKs / TANF (cash for families)
- **California:** Your county social services office, or online
  - [benefitscal.com](https://benefitscal.com) ✓
- **Other states:** Your state TANF agency
  - [ACF Help for Families (state TANF contacts)](https://acf.gov/ofa/map/about/help-families) (page exists per search; blocks automated checks)
- **Bring:** ID, Social Security numbers and birth certificates for everyone, kids' shot records, proof of income and rent
- **How long:** Up to 45 days. "Immediate Need" cash can come within 1 day in a crisis.
- **Tip:** Homeless or facing eviction? Ask for CalWORKs Homeless Assistance (temporary shelter at $85/day).

### General Relief (county cash for adults) (California only)
- **California:** Your county social services office (LA County: DPSS)
  - [benefitscal.com](https://benefitscal.com) ✓
  - Phone: LA County DPSS: (866) 613-3777
- **Bring:** ID, proof you live in the county, any income proof (or a statement that you have none)
- **How long:** Varies by county

### SSI / SSP (65+, blind or disabled)
- **California:** Social Security: online, by phone or at a local office
  - [ssa.gov/ssi](https://www.ssa.gov/ssi) (site blocks automated checks; open in a browser)
  - Phone: 1-800-772-1213
- **Other states:** Social Security (same everywhere)
  - [ssa.gov/ssi](https://www.ssa.gov/ssi) (site blocks automated checks; open in a browser)
  - Phone: 1-800-772-1213
- **Bring:** Birth certificate, ID, Social Security card, names of your doctors and medical records, bank statements, lease
- **How long:** Several months. Disability decisions often take longer.
- **Tip:** Call first to set up an appointment. The date you call can count as your filing date.

### CAPI (cash for immigrants) (California only)
- **California:** Your county social services office (form SOC 814). Social Security must first have said no to SSI because of your immigration status.
- **Bring:** Immigration documents, the SSI denial letter, ID, income and bank statements
- **How long:** Varies by county

### Unemployment
- **California:** EDD, online through UI Online / myEDD
  - [edd.ca.gov](https://edd.ca.gov) ✓
  - Phone: 1-800-300-5616
- **Other states:** Your state's unemployment office
  - [CareerOneStop unemployment finder](https://www.careeronestop.org) ✓
- **Bring:** Social Security number, ID, 18 months of employer names, dates and pay, bank info
- **How long:** File the first week you're out of work. EDD mails your claim details within about 10 days.

## Health

### Medi-Cal / Medicaid
- **California:** Your county social services office, or online at either site
  - [benefitscal.com](https://benefitscal.com) ✓
  - [coveredca.com](https://www.coveredca.com) ✓
  - Phone: Medi-Cal: (800) 541-5555
- **Other states:** Your state Medicaid agency. HealthCare.gov will screen you and send your application there.
  - [healthcare.gov](https://www.healthcare.gov) ✓
  - Phone: 1-800-318-2596
- **Bring:** ID, Social Security numbers, 1 month of pay stubs, proof of address, immigration papers if you're not a citizen
- **How long:** Up to 45 days (90 with a disability). A hospital can sign you up for temporary coverage the same day.
- **Tip:** Coverage can reach back up to 3 months to pay old medical bills (applications before 2027; less after).

### Pregnancy coverage
- **California:** At your first prenatal visit, ask for "presumptive eligibility" (same-day coverage), then apply for Medi-Cal or MCAP
  - [coveredca.com](https://www.coveredca.com) ✓
  - Phone: (800) 541-5555
- **Other states:** Your clinic (ask for presumptive eligibility) or your state Medicaid office
  - [healthcare.gov](https://www.healthcare.gov) ✓
- **Bring:** Proof of pregnancy if asked, ID, income proof
- **How long:** Temporary coverage the same day. Lasts 12 months after the birth.

### Kids' health coverage
- **California:** Kids go on Medi-Cal: apply the same way as Medi-Cal
  - [benefitscal.com](https://benefitscal.com) ✓
  - Phone: (800) 541-5555
- **Other states:** Medicaid / CHIP in your state
  - [insurekidsnow.gov](https://www.insurekidsnow.gov) (site blocks automated checks; open in a browser)
  - Phone: 1-877-543-7669 (KIDS-NOW)
- **Bring:** Child's birth certificate, income proof, proof of address
- **How long:** Up to 45 days

### Marketplace insurance with a subsidy
- **California:** Covered California, online or with a free certified enroller near you
  - [coveredca.com](https://www.coveredca.com) ✓
  - Phone: (800) 300-1506
- **Other states:** HealthCare.gov, or free local help
  - [healthcare.gov](https://www.healthcare.gov) ✓
  - [localhelp.healthcare.gov](https://localhelp.healthcare.gov) ✓
  - Phone: 1-800-318-2596
- **Bring:** Social Security numbers, an income estimate (last tax return or W-2), any job-based insurance offer
- **How long:** Open enrollment starts Nov 1, 2026. At other times you need a life event (job loss, move, birth, marriage).

### Medicare Savings Programs
- **California:** Your county social services office. HICAP gives free help applying.
  - [benefitscal.com](https://benefitscal.com) ✓
  - Phone: HICAP: 1-800-434-0222
- **Other states:** Your state Medicaid office. Your SHIP office gives free help.
  - [shiphelp.org](https://www.shiphelp.org) ✓
- **Bring:** Medicare card, ID, income proof, bank statements
- **How long:** About 45 days

### Extra Help (drug costs)
- **California:** Social Security, online or by phone
  - [ssa.gov Extra Help](https://www.ssa.gov/medicare/part-d-extra-help) (site blocks automated checks; open in a browser)
  - Phone: 1-800-772-1213
- **Other states:** Social Security (same everywhere)
  - [ssa.gov Extra Help](https://www.ssa.gov/medicare/part-d-extra-help) (site blocks automated checks; open in a browser)
  - Phone: 1-800-772-1213
- **Bring:** Social Security number, bank statements, pension and tax info
- **How long:** A few weeks
- **Tip:** You get it automatically if you have Medi-Cal, SSI or a Medicare Savings Program.

### In-home care (IHSS)
- **California:** Your county IHSS office (form SOC 295). You need Medi-Cal, so apply for both.
- **Other states:** Your state Medicaid agency (home care or waiver programs). The Eldercare Locator can point you there.
  - [eldercare.acl.gov](https://eldercare.acl.gov) ✓
  - Phone: 1-800-677-1116
- **Bring:** Medi-Cal info, ID, income proof, a health form signed by your doctor
- **How long:** Up to 90 days, including a home visit

### Free family planning (Family PACT) (California only)
- **California:** Walk into any Family PACT clinic. You sign up on the spot.
  - [familypact.org](https://familypact.org) ✓
- **Bring:** Nothing required. Bring ID if you have it.
- **How long:** Same day

### Free or low-cost clinics
- **California:** Community health centers (sliding fees). In LA County, county clinics are free up to 200% of the poverty line ("Ability to Pay").
  - [Find a Health Center](https://findahealthcenter.hrsa.gov) ✓
  - Phone: 211
- **Other states:** Community health centers with sliding fees
  - [Find a Health Center](https://findahealthcenter.hrsa.gov) ✓
- **Bring:** Income proof (or a statement of no income), ID
- **How long:** Often the same week

## Housing

### Section 8 vouchers and public housing
- **California:** Your city or county housing authority. Join every open waitlist near you.
  - [HACLA (LA city)](https://www.hacla.org) ✓
  - [LACDA (LA County)](https://www.lacda.org) ✓
  - [San Diego (SDHC)](https://www.sdhc.org) (site blocks automated checks; open in a browser)
  - [San Francisco](https://www.sfha.org) ✓
  - [Oakland](https://www.oakha.org) ✓
  - [Sacramento waitlist](https://sacwaitlist.com/landing) ✓
- **Other states:** Your local public housing authority (PHA)
  - [HUD](https://www.hud.gov) ✓
- **Bring:** ID, Social Security numbers and birth certificates for everyone, income and bank statements
- **How long:** Waitlists are often years long, and many open for only a few days
- **Tip:** Sign up for each housing authority's email alerts and keep your address current, or you can be dropped from the list.

### Affordable (below-market) apartments
- **California:** Housing lotteries and listings
  - [SF DAHLIA](https://housing.sfgov.org) ✓
  - [LA County Housing Resource Center](https://housing.lacounty.gov) ✓
  - Phone: LA County: 1-877-428-8844
- **Other states:** Local listings and HUD's resource locator
  - [resources.hud.gov](https://resources.hud.gov) ✓
- **Bring:** Income proof and ID for everyone
- **How long:** Apply to each listing before its deadline

### Homeless help and eviction defense
- **California:** Call or text 211 for shelter and Coordinated Entry. In LA, Stay Housed LA helps with evictions.
  - [stayhousedla.org](https://www.stayhousedla.org) ✓
  - Phone: 211
  - Phone: Stay Housed LA: (888) 694-0040
- **Other states:** 211, or HUD's shelter finder
  - [211.org](https://www.211.org) ✓
  - [HUD Find Shelter](https://www.hud.gov/findshelter) ✓
  - Phone: 211
- **Bring:** Any eviction notice or court papers
- **How long:** Act right away. In California you have 10 court days to answer an eviction lawsuit.

## Bills and phone

### Energy bill help (HEAP / LIHEAP)
- **California:** Your local energy agency. Find it on the CSD site by ZIP code.
  - [CSD: find services](https://www.csd.ca.gov/Pages/FindServicesInYourArea.aspx) ✓
- **Other states:** Your state LIHEAP office
  - [LIHEAP Clearinghouse](https://liheapch.ncat.org) ✓
  - Phone: Energy assistance referral: 1-866-674-6327
- **Bring:** Recent gas and electric bills, income proof for every adult, ID
- **How long:** Weeks. Faster if you have a shutoff notice, so say so when you call.

### Weatherization
- **California:** The same local energy agency as HEAP
  - [CSD: find services](https://www.csd.ca.gov/Pages/FindServicesInYourArea.aspx) ✓
- **Other states:** Your state weatherization program
  - [energy.gov: how to apply](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/scep/wap/how-apply-weatherization-assistance) ✓
- **Bring:** Income proof, ID, your landlord's OK if you rent
- **How long:** Often a waitlist

### CARE and FERA bill discounts (California only)
- **California:** A short form on your utility's website
  - [PG&E](https://www.pge.com/care) ✓
  - [SCE](https://www.sce.com/save-money/income-qualified-programs/care-fera) ✓
  - [SDG&E](https://www.sdge.com) ✓
  - [SoCalGas](https://www.socalgas.com) ✓
  - [LADWP EZ-SAVE](https://www.ladwp.com/residential-services/assistance-programs/ez-save-program) ✓
  - [SMUD](https://www.smud.org/EAPRapp) ✓
- **Bring:** Your account number and household size. Proof is only needed if you're picked for verification.
- **How long:** Minutes online. The discount shows up on an upcoming bill.
- **Tip:** Being on Medi-Cal, CalFresh, SSI or WIC automatically qualifies you for CARE.

### Lifeline phone/internet discount
- **California:** Through a phone or internet company that offers California LifeLine
  - [californialifeline.com](https://www.californialifeline.com) (site blocks automated checks; open in a browser)
  - Phone: 1-866-272-0349
- **Other states:** Through a participating phone company, or online with the federal National Verifier
  - [lifelinesupport.org](https://www.lifelinesupport.org) (site blocks automated checks; open in a browser)
  - Phone: 1-800-234-9473
- **Bring:** Your Medi-Cal, CalFresh or SSI card or letter, or income proof
- **How long:** Often the same day

## Kids and school

### Head Start / Early Head Start
- **California:** A local Head Start program
  - [Head Start locator](https://headstart.gov) ✓
- **Other states:** A local Head Start program
  - [Head Start locator](https://headstart.gov) ✓
- **Bring:** Child's birth certificate, income proof, proof of address, shot records
- **How long:** Waitlists are common, so apply early

### Child care help
- **California:** On CalWORKs? Ask your county worker. Otherwise go to your local Resource & Referral agency.
  - [rrnetwork.org](https://rrnetwork.org) ✓
- **Other states:** Your state's child care assistance program
  - [childcare.gov](https://childcare.gov) ✓
- **Bring:** Work or school schedule, income proof, kids' birth certificates
- **How long:** Waitlists are common outside CalWORKs

### State Preschool and Transitional Kindergarten (California only)
- **California:** Your school district's enrollment office. TK is free for kids who turn 4 by Sept 1.
- **Bring:** Child's birth certificate, proof of address, shot records (and income proof for State Preschool)
- **How long:** Enroll in spring for fall

### College aid (Pell, Cal Grant, fee waiver)
- **California:** FAFSA, or the CA Dream Act application if you're undocumented. Your college's financial aid office can help.
  - [studentaid.gov](https://studentaid.gov) ✓
  - [dream.csac.ca.gov](https://dream.csac.ca.gov) ✓
  - Phone: FAFSA: 1-800-433-3243
- **Other states:** FAFSA
  - [studentaid.gov](https://studentaid.gov) ✓
  - Phone: 1-800-433-3243
- **Bring:** FSA ID, your (and parents') tax info, Social Security number
- **How long:** Cal Grant deadline: March 2, 2027. Community college students can apply until Sept 2.

## Taxes and legal

### Tax credits (EITC, Child Tax Credit, CalEITC, Young Child Tax Credit)
- **California:** Free tax prep at a VITA site, or file yourself with CalFile (state return)
  - [IRS VITA locator](https://irs.treasury.gov/freetaxprep) ✓
  - [CalEITC4Me](https://caleitc4me.org) ✓
  - [ftb.ca.gov](https://www.ftb.ca.gov) ✓
  - Phone: VITA: 1-800-906-9887
- **Other states:** Free tax prep at a VITA site, or IRS Free File
  - [IRS VITA locator](https://irs.treasury.gov/freetaxprep) ✓
  - [IRS Free File](https://www.irs.gov/freefile) ✓
  - Phone: 1-800-906-9887
- **Bring:** Photo ID, Social Security cards for everyone, W-2s and 1099s, last year's return, bank info for direct deposit
- **How long:** Refunds arrive about 3 weeks after e-filing (filing season: Jan to Apr 15)
- **Tip:** File even if you earned too little to owe tax. That's the only way to get these credits.

### Free legal help
- **California:** A legal aid office near you
  - [lawhelpca.org](https://www.lawhelpca.org) (site blocks automated checks; open in a browser)
  - [immigrationlawhelp.org](https://www.immigrationlawhelp.org) ✓
- **Other states:** A legal aid office near you
  - [lawhelp.org](https://www.lawhelp.org) (site blocks automated checks; open in a browser)
  - [LSC: get legal help](https://www.lsc.gov/about-lsc/what-legal-aid/get-legal-help) ✓
- **Bring:** Every notice, letter or court paper you've received, ID, income proof
- **How long:** Call the same day you get court papers
