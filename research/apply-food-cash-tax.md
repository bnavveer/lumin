## 1. SNAP / CalFresh

- **CA online:** BenefitsCal, https://www.benefitscal.com (fetch returned 403; URL cited in CDSS outreach snippet, https://cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/CalFresh-Outreach/Upcoming-Events)
- **CA phone:** CalFresh Benefits Helpline 1-877-847-3663. Listed on CDSS contacts page, https://www.cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/CalFresh/Contacts. Apply-by-phone option cited from an LACC page (secondary). Hours (Mon-Fri 8-5) **UNCONFIRMED**.
- **CA in person:** Your county social services / human services office. Find it through the CalFresh county contact list on https://www.cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/CalFresh/Contacts (**UNCONFIRMED**: exact page content not fetched).
- **CA mobile app:** None verified. BenefitsCal is web-based.
- **Other states:** USDA FNS State Directory, https://www.fns.usda.gov/snap/state-directory. Click your state on the map to get contact and application info. FNS does not take applications itself (FNS applicant page, https://www.fns.usda.gov/snap/applicant-participant).
- **Bring:** ID; proof of income (pay stubs, self-employment records); proof of rent/utilities/housing costs; bank statements; Social Security numbers for household members; immigration documents if applicable. Verification list **UNCONFIRMED** against an official page.
- **Timing:**
  - Standard: decision within 30 days of application (CDSS standard, cited via expedited FAQs at https://www.cdss.ca.gov/lettersnotices/entres/getinfo/acl/2014/14-20.pdf).
  - Expedited (ES): federal rule is 7 days (7 CFR 273.2(i)(3)(i), cited in search summary). California's 2016 transmittal says benefits must be accessible by the **3rd calendar day** after application, with counties making every effort to meet 7 days (https://www.cdss.ca.gov/lettersnotices/entres/getinfo/acl/2016/16-14.pdf). Eligibility for ES: very low income or under $150 in liquid resources (**UNCONFIRMED**, not fetched).
  - **Interview:** Required. For expedited cases the county schedules the interview within the ES window. If you miss it, the county sends a Notice of Missed Interview (NOMI). Answer calls from unknown numbers during the application window.
- **Tip:** Apply online or by phone the same day, even if you lack documents. Submit what you have; the county will request the rest.

## 2. WIC

- **CA online:** MyFamily site, https://www.myfamily.wic.ca.gov. Use the "Find Your WIC Office" button to locate your local agency (page fetched: https://www.myfamily.wic.ca.gov/Home/ContactUs). Online application link **UNCONFIRMED**.
- **CA phone:** California WIC Family Services 1-800-852-5770, Mon-Fri 8 a.m.-5 p.m. (https://www.myfamily.wic.ca.gov/Home/ContactUs). WIC Card customer service 1-844-469-3264, 24/7 (same page).
- **CA in person:** Local WIC agency. Find it on the MyFamily "Find Your WIC Office" search by ZIP code (https://www.myfamily.wic.ca.gov/Home/ContactUs). Call the office for an appointment.
- **CA mobile app:** "WIC App" (part of "WIC Card and WIC App," https://www.myfamily.wic.ca.gov/Home/ContactUs).
- **Other states:** USDA WIC locator, https://www.fns.usda.gov/wic/locator, and How to Apply, https://www.fns.usda.gov/wic/apply (USDA says contact a local WIC agency by phone or online and schedule an appointment). signupwic.com was referenced by a PA WIC site as the place to find a program **UNCONFIRMED** (site not reached). State WIC contacts page https://www.fns.usda.gov/wic/wic-state-agency-contacts returned 403 **UNCONFIRMED**.
- **Bring:** Proof of identity for the parent or guardian; proof of residence; proof of income or Medi-Cal/CalFresh/CalWORKs enrollment (if enrolled, you can usually qualify with that alone) (**UNCONFIRMED** list, not fetched); child's birth certificate or ID; pregnancy/medical info if applicable.
- **Timing:** Appointment-based. Same-day certification is common but processing time **UNCONFIRMED**.
- **Tip:** Calling the local office directly for an appointment is usually faster than waiting for statewide lines.

## 3. Free School Meals and SUN Bucks (Summer EBT)

### School meals (California)
- **CA online:** No separate application for universal meals. CDE FAQ: "there is not a separate application specifically for the California Universal Meals Program" and eligibility covers all public school students with no income test. https://www.cde.ca.gov/Ls/nu/univmealsfaq.asp
- **CA phone:** Contact your school district's nutrition services office (**UNCONFIRMED**, number varies by district).
- **CA in person:** Your school site.
- **CA mobile app:** None.
- **Other states:** Ask your school district or the state school nutrition agency. The USDA free/reduced-price application (school meal application) is used by non-universal states (**UNCONFIRMED** link).
- **Form question:** The "Universal Benefits Application" and "Alternative Income Form" are mentioned in county notices for SUN Bucks, not for school meals. Whether you should submit one is **UNCONFIRMED**.
- **Bring:** Nothing for universal meals. For the optional Multipurpose Family Income Form, household income info (SFUSD press release, https://www.sfusd.edu/vi/node/13752, secondary).
- **Timing:** Meals start when school starts; no application wait.

### SUN Bucks (Summer EBT)
- **Program overview:** $40/month, June-August 2026 ($120/child). Announced May 21, 2026: https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/05/21/california-launches-sun-bucks-for-summer-2026-to-help-fight-childhood-food-insecurity/
- **Automatic enrollment:** Children in CalFresh, CalWORKs, or Medi-Cal households are generally enrolled automatically (San Bernardino County notice, https://main.sbcounty.gov/?p=184905). Children certified for free/reduced school meals are generally enrolled automatically (USDA summary, https://www.fns.usda.gov/summer/sunbucks).
- **CA online / app:** ebtEDGE app and ebt.ca.gov/cardholder to set a PIN (per San Bernardino County notice). Official CA SUN Bucks page: cdss.ca.gov/sun-bucks (cited in county notice; **UNCONFIRMED**, not fetched). sunbucks.ca.gov was **not found**.
- **CA phone:** SUN Bucks Helpline 877-328-9677 (San Bernardino County notice; **UNCONFIRMED** against official page).
- **CA in person:** Your county human services office, or the school. Not verified.
- **Other states:** USDA SUN Bucks page, https://www.fns.usda.gov/summer/sunbucks, and state agency list at https://www.fns.usda.gov/sebt/state-agencies (**UNCONFIRMED**, not fetched). sunbucks.gov was **not verified**.
- **2027 timeline:** **UNCONFIRMED**. No official 2027 California date found.
- **Bring:** Child's name and DOB; household info if applying directly (Universal Benefits Application **UNCONFIRMED**).
- **Timing:** Benefits are loaded on a card in June-July. Cards arrive by mail. Use within 122 days of issuance (county notice). Unused benefits expire and cannot be replaced.
- **Tip:** If your child is in CalFresh, CalWORKs, or Medi-Cal, you do not need to apply.

## 5. TANF / CalWORKs

- **CA online:** BenefitsCal, https://www.benefitscal.com (CalWORKs can be filed there, per county pages; search snippet). County pages also list it: Sacramento, https://ha.saccounty.gov/benefits/CalWORKs/Pages/default.aspx.
- **CA phone:** County-specific. Not verified.
- **CA in person:** Your county human/social services office. San Diego requires a face-to-face interview at a Family Resource Center even for online filing (https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/content/sdc/hhsa/programs/ssp/calworks/how_to_apply.html).
- **Immediate Need (homeless assistance):** Available when you have an emergency: homelessness, eviction notice, utility shutoff, or food running out in 3 days. Alameda, https://socialservices.alamedacountyca.gov/our-services/Work-and-Money/CalWORKs/immediate-need. Ventura lists a $200 max payment (https://venturacounty.gov/human-services-agency/?p=392277). San Luis Obispo covers shelter costs (https://www.slocounty.ca.gov/departments/social-services/cash-assistance-programs/services/homeless-assistance).
- **CA mobile app:** None verified.
- **Other states:** ACF "Help for Families" directory, https://acf.gov/ofa/help. Select your state for TANF contacts. Current state phone numbers **UNCONFIRMED** (the archived directory was outdated).
- **Bring:** ID; Social Security numbers; proof of income; proof of residence; proof of homelessness or emergency (for Immediate Need).
- **Timing:** Immediate Need: processed in 1 day (Sacramento) or 24 hours (Alameda). Standard CalWORKs timing **UNCONFIRMED**.
- **Tip:** Ask specifically for "Immediate Need" at intake. You can request it before the full application is complete.

## 7. SSI / SSP and CAPI

### SSI (federal, via SSA)
- **CA online:** Online SSI application is available for some applicants, per SSA blog (https://blog.ssa.gov/social-security-administration-announces-new-efforts-to-simplify-ssi-applications/comment-page-1; the post says streamlined online SSI started Dec 2024). Eligibility details for online filing **UNCONFIRMED** (ssa.gov page returned 403). Per an archived SSA page, adults 65+ cannot file online and must call or visit (**UNCONFIRMED**).
- **CA phone:** SSA national line 1-800-772-1213, Mon-Fri 8 a.m.-7 p.m. (2024 SSA blog post, same URL above; **UNCONFIRMED** against current ssa.gov).
- **CA in person:** SSA office locator, https://secure.ssa.gov/ICON/main.jsp (**UNCONFIRMED**, not fetched).
- **CA mobile app:** my Social Security app (**UNCONFIRMED**).
- **Other states:** Same SSA national line and office locator.
- **Bring:** SSN; birth certificate; ID; medical records and provider names; income and asset info; bank records.
- **Timing:** Processing time conflicts across sources. One Kentucky guide says four months or more; a Nevada senator's page says up to two years (both secondary, **UNCONFIRMED**). Approved benefits can be paid back to the application date.

### CAPI (state program, county-run)
- **CA online:** Varies by county. Yolo County lists BenefitsCal (https://www.yolocounty.gov/government/general-government-departments/health-human-services/welfare/cash-assistance-program-for-immigrants-capi). El Dorado says not available on BenefitsCal. Verify with your county.
- **CA form:** SOC 814, from CDSS (https://cdss.ca.gov/capi). Submit by mail, fax, in person, or email to county.
- **CA phone / in person:** Your county social services office. Calaveras forwards to Sacramento County for processing (https://hhsa.calaverasgov.us/HHSA/Human-Services/Public-Assistance/Cash-Assistance-Program-for-Immigrants).
- **Eligibility:** Age 65+, blind, or disabled; resources limit $2,000 individual / $3,000 couple (Yolo County, secondary, **UNCONFIRMED**).
- **Bring:** Completed SOC 814; ID; income and asset documents; immigration documents.
- **Timing:** Not verified.

## 8. CFAP (California Food Assistance Program)

- **CA online:** Through county / BenefitsCal, per Fresno County (https://www.fresnocountyca.gov/Departments/Social-Services/Assistance-Programs/CalFresh/California-Food-Assistance-Program-CFAP). BenefitsCal-specific steps **UNCONFIRMED**.
- **CA phone / in person:** Your county social services office; CDSS CFAP page https://cdss.ca.gov/inforesources/cdss-programs/calfresh/cfap (**UNCONFIRMED**).
- **CA mobile app:** None verified.
- **Other states:** Not applicable (CA only).
- **Eligibility:** Qualified immigrants ineligible for CalFresh due to 1996 welfare-reform rules. Expansion to all income-eligible Californians age 55+ is anticipated October 1, 2027 (CDSS, cited via search; **UNCONFIRMED** date).
- **Bring:** Same as CalFresh, plus immigration documents.
- **Timing:** Same as CalFresh (30 days standard; expedited rules apply).
- **Tip:** CFAP benefits are issued on the same case as CalFresh.

## 9. Head Start / Early Head Start

- **CA online:** Head Start Locator, https://headstart.gov (see video guide https://headstart.gov/video/how-use-head-start-locator; how to apply https://www.headstart.gov/how-apply).
- **CA phone:** 1-866-763-6481, Mon-Fri 8 a.m.-6 p.m. ET (headstart.gov search snippet; **UNCONFIRMED** against the official page).
- **CA in person:** The local Head Start program; locate it via the Head Start Locator.
- **CA mobile app:** None.
- **Other states:** Same Head Start Locator and phone line.
- **Eligibility:** Generally family income at or below poverty guidelines; foster care, homeless, and public-assistance families are eligible regardless of income.
- **Bring:** Child's birth certificate; proof of income; proof of address; immunization records (**UNCONFIRMED**, local program lists its own).
- **Timing:** Waitlists are common. Enrollment timing **UNCONFIRMED**.
- **Tip:** Ask the local program to add your child to the waitlist if no seat is open.

- **Tip:** Free tax prep sites only serve income-eligible filers; check the site before going.
