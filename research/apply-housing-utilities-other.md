## 1. Section 8 Housing Choice Voucher and Public Housing

**Key fact:** Housing Choice Vouchers (Section 8) and public housing are run by local Public Housing Agencies (PHAs). You apply to your local PHA, not to HUD. Some PHAs have long waitlists, so applying to more than one can help. [HUD Contact Us, via search: https://www.hud.gov/contactus]

### California

- **Online application:** Each PHA has its own portal. Verified portals and status:
  - **HACLA (Los Angeles):** Applicant portal listed at https://publichousing-hacla.securecafe.com/onlineleasing/la-live/guestlogin.aspx. The page does not say this portal is for the Housing Choice Voucher waitlist, so it may be for public housing only. Waitlist open/closed: **UNCONFIRMED**. Source: https://www.hacla.org
  - **LACDA (Los Angeles County):** No application URL found. General phone (626) 262-4511, footer of agency site, not specific to Section 8. The page says some public housing site-based waitlists are closed. Section 8 waitlist open/closed: **UNCONFIRMED**. Source: https://www.lacda.org
  - **San Diego Housing Commission:** https://www.sdhc.org returned HTTP 403. URL, phone, and waitlist status: **UNCONFIRMED**.
  - **SF Housing Authority:** Office at 1815 Egbert Avenue, San Francisco, CA 94124, Mon–Fri 8:00 AM–5:00 PM. Waitlist page is https://www.sfha.org/housing-programs/waitlist, but its contents were not retrieved. Phone and waitlist status: **UNCONFIRMED**. Source: https://www.sfha.org
  - **Oakland Housing Authority:** The guessed domain oakhaus.org does not resolve. Correct URL, phone, and waitlist status: **UNCONFIRMED**.
  - **Sacramento SHRA:** All waitlists are posted only on Sacwaitlist.com. Apply at https://sacwaitlist.com/landing (general site https://www.sacwaitlist.com/home). Phone not listed. Current open/closed status: **UNCONFIRMED**. Source: https://www.shra.org
  - Warning from SHRA: flyers advertising Section 8 application help from "www.govassistance.org" are not legitimate. Source: https://www.shra.org
- **Phone:** Find the local PHA through HUD's contact directory or call HUD at (800) 955-2232. Source: https://www.hud.gov/contactus (found by search, page not fetched directly; the state table on that page has known errors, so confirm each PHA's own phone).
- **In person:** Find the local PHA office by searching the HUD PHA directory (see Other states below) or the city/county name plus "housing authority." Office hours and addresses must be confirmed on each PHA's site.
- **Waitlist alerts:** AffordableHousing.com and each PHA's own email list. Email alerts from PHAs are typically set up on their website. Specific sign-up URLs: **UNCONFIRMED**.

### Other states

- **Locator:** HUD's PHA contact directory at https://www.hud.gov/program_offices/public_indian_housing/pha/contacts (the page fetched did not contain a directory; use https://www.hud.gov/contactus or call HUD at (800) 955-2232 to get the state list). Source: https://www.hud.gov/contactus
- **Homeless veterans:** HUD-VASH pairs vouchers with VA services. VA line: 1-877-424-3838 (24/7). Source: search result citing HUD/VA guidance (not fetched directly).

### What to bring
- Household members' names, birthdates, Social Security numbers
- Income and asset information for everyone in the household (pay stubs, benefit letters)
- Identification and proof of residence where listed (verify list per PHA)

Source: https://www.hud.gov/contactus (general: "You will need to provide information about your family's income, assets, and household members.")

### How long it takes
- Waitlists can be long, and PHAs open and close them. Typical wait times: **UNCONFIRMED** for each PHA.

### Tip
Apply to every PHA whose area you can live in. Keep a free email alert set up on each PHA site, and check for "application period" notices.

## 3. Homeless Help and Eviction Help

### California and Los Angeles County

- **211:** Free, confidential, 24/7 in more than 150 languages. Call 2-1-1 or text the keyword to 211-211. Browsable directory at 211.org. Source: https://www.pge.com/211 (describes 211 California service and text routes); https://www.pge.com/assets/pge/docs/outages-and-safety/outage-preparedness-and-support/211-fact-sheet.pdf. Official 211ca.org / 211la.org URLs: **UNCONFIRMED**.
- **Stay Housed LA (eviction help):** Hotline (888) 694-0040. Website: stayhousedla.org. Free legal help for eligible tenants, including tenant rights and help filing an Answer. Source: https://homeless.lacounty.gov/news/stayhoused/ (phone number confirmed in search results; page not fetched directly).
  - **Timing:** If you have been served an unlawful detainer (eviction lawsuit), you have only **5 business days** to file an Answer. Source: South Pasadena housing legal resources PDF (secondary source). Official confirmation: **UNCONFIRMED**.
- **We Are LA (Mayor's Fund) hotline:** 213-584-1808. Helps renters avoid eviction and file an Answer. Source: search result (Mayor's Fund site); **UNCONFIRMED** on the official page.
- **LA County Coordinated Entry / LAHSA:** Coordinated Entry System (CES) URL and phone: **UNCONFIRMED**.
- **CalWORKs Homeless Assistance:** Apply through your county welfare department. Not verified during this research beyond general county administration. Details: **UNCONFIRMED**.

### Other states

- **211.org:** Find your state or local 211 by zip code. Source: https://www.aarp.org/aarp-foundation/find-help/housing/affordable-housing/emergency-senior-housing/ (secondary; official 211.org not fetched).
- **HUD Find Shelter:** https://www.hud.gov/findshelter. Mapping and contact info for shelters, clinics, and food pantries. Launched January 2021. Source: https://www.nhhfa.org/?p=22894 (secondary; official page not fetched).

### What to bring
- For eviction: the eviction notice and any court papers (summons, complaint) as soon as you receive them.
- For shelter: ID if you have it; the 211 operator will tell you what to bring for each provider.

### How long it takes
- 211 is available any time, 24/7.
- Eviction Answer: 5 business days after service of an unlawful detainer (secondary source; verify).

### Tip
If you get court papers, call Stay Housed LA or a legal aid group the same day. The 5-day deadline is short.

## 4. LIHEAP / HEAP and Weatherization

### California

- **Find your local provider:** CSD "Find Services in Your Area": https://www.csd.ca.gov/Pages/FindServicesInYourArea.aspx. Search by county, city, or ZIP. Source: same page.
- **CSD call center:** (866) 675-6623, with free translation and interpreter services. Source: search result citing csd.ca.gov Language Resources page; **not confirmed on the CSD contact page**. Treat as **UNCONFIRMED**. Another source lists 800-433-4327; treat as **UNCONFIRMED** too.
- **How to apply:** Applications go through the local provider, not a state portal. HEAP gives a one-time payment toward an energy bill, and households may apply once per year. Source: CSD search result (secondary).
- **Weatherization:** Run at state and local level. Find your local agency through the state's "How to Apply" listing of providers (organized by county). Source: https://www.energy.gov/cmei/scep/wap/how-apply-weatherization-assistance (search result; page not fetched). Weatherization eligibility generally follows federal poverty guidelines. Source: https://www.energy.gov/cmei/scep/wap/weatherization-assistance-program
- **In person:** The local community action agency or local provider listed in the CSD finder.

### Other states

- **LIHEAP Clearinghouse / state contacts:** https://liheapch.ncat.org/node/584 (state director contacts section). Source: search result.
- **NEAR hotline:** 1-866-674-6327 (toll-free). It connects households with their local LIHEAP office. Source: NCAT search results (current NCAT pages). The federal government does not take LIHEAP applications. Email address for NCAT energy assistance: **UNCONFIRMED** (sources list two different addresses).
- **Weatherization locator:** Start at https://www.energy.gov/cmei/scep/wap/how-apply-weatherization-assistance and use the state map. National EERE Information Center: 1-877-337-3463. Source: DOE fact sheet (secondary).

### What to bring
- Recent bills (energy and heating), proof of income for the prior year (pay stubs, Social Security or benefit letters), and proof of household members. Source: https://www.energy.gov/cmei/scep/wap/how-apply-weatherization-assistance (general "proof of income" guidance).

### How long it takes
- Emergency help: Ask the local provider about urgent or shutoff assistance. Specific timing: **UNCONFIRMED**.
- Weatherization wait times vary. **UNCONFIRMED**.

### Tip
If a shutoff notice arrives, call the utility and your local provider the same day. Ask for emergency or crisis assistance.

From February 1, 2026, Lifeline eligibility is checked through the **National Verifier**. Older guides describing a state application process are outdated. Source: https://benton.org/node/355981 (secondary, FCC announcement summary); https://www.benton.org/headlines/national-lifeline-eligibility-verifier-launches-california

Application/renewal questions: 1-877-858-7463 (California LifeLine Administrator). Spanish: 1-866-272-0350. Sources disagree on which line handles applications. **UNCONFIRMED** on the CPUC page.

## 7. Child Care Help

### California
- **Find child care / R&R agency:** mychildcareplan.org (statewide finder). Not fetched; **UNCONFIRMED.** Use your county's Child Care Resource and Referral (R&R) agency. Statewide number 1-800-KIDS-793 (per search result). **UNCONFIRMED.**
- **CalWORKs child care:** Apply through your county welfare department (in person, by phone, or in writing). Program info: https://www.cdss.ca.gov/calworks-child-care. Current or former CalWORKs cash aid recipients who are working or in approved Welfare-to-Work activities may qualify. Children up to age 12 (up to 21 with exceptional needs). Source: search results (CDSS page and county pages).
- **If not on cash aid:** Contact the local R&R or Alternative Payment Program (APP) in your county.
- **California State Preschool and TK:** Apply through your local school district. CDE: **UNCONFIRMED** (not verified during this research).

### Other states
- **childcare.gov:** Use the "Find Child Care" page to reach your state's child care search. Then open your state's "Financial Assistance Resources for Families" page. Example: https://childcare.gov/state-resources/california/financial-assistance-resources-for-families (California page exists; contents not fetched). Source: childcare.gov search results.

### What to bring
- Proof of work or training schedule, income, household members, and children's birth certificates (typical requirements; **UNCONFIRMED** for each county).

### How long it takes
- **UNCONFIRMED.** Waits for subsidized child care can be long.

### Tip
If you are on CalWORKs, ask your caseworker for a child care referral. It can be faster than applying cold.

## 8. College Aid (FAFSA, CA Dream Act, Cal Grant)

### Federal (FAFSA)
- **Apply:** https://studentaid.gov (FAFSA). Phone: 1-800-433-3243. Phone number: **UNCONFIRMED** (official studentaid.gov page timed out during this research).
- **Opening date:** The 2027–28 FAFSA is already open. It is the earliest launch in the program's history (for students starting classes July 1, 2027). The exact opening day was not found. Source: news syndicate story (multiple outlets). Verify on studentaid.gov.
- **Completion time:** Average about 15 minutes per Under Secretary Nicholas Kent (per news story).
- **What to bring:** Your Social Security number, FSA ID (create at studentaid.gov), prior-year tax info (or the IRS Direct Data Exchange), bank balances, and investment info. **UNCONFIRMED** on the official page.

### California
- **CA Dream Act Application (CADAA):** Opens October 1. Priority deadline March 2, 2027 (for the 2027–28 cycle). Final deadline June 30. Source: scholarship and college pages (secondary). Official URL https://dream.csac.ca.gov: **UNCONFIRMED** (not reached directly).
- **Cal Grant:** Requires CADAA (or FAFSA) and a certified GPA sent to the California Student Aid Commission by March 2. A new GPA form is needed each cycle. Source: college financial aid pages (secondary). **UNCONFIRMED** on the official page.
- **Community college exception:** CA resident community college students who miss March 2 can apply by September 2 for competitive Cal Grant. Source: secondary.
- **California College Promise Grant (fee waiver):** icanaffordcollege.com or your college's financial aid office. **UNCONFIRMED.**
- **In person:** Your college's financial aid office.

### What to bring
- FAFSA or CADAA details, tax info (prior-year), Social Security number (for FAFSA), and GPA transcript for Cal Grant.

### How long it takes
- FAFSA: about 15 minutes to complete (news story). Processing time: **UNCONFIRMED.**

### Tip
Apply by March 2 even if you are unsure. The Cal Grant priority date is strict, and the CADAA is needed for both state and college aid.

## 10. Unemployment Insurance

### California (EDD)
- **Apply online:** https://edd.ca.gov/en/unemployment/ui_online/ (UI Online). Registration uses myEDD. Source: EDD search results.
- **Phone:** 1-800-300-5616, Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–5 p.m. Pacific, except state holidays. Use this for applying for benefits. Source: https://edd.ca.gov/en/unemployment/contact_ui/ (search result).
- **UI Online technical help:** 1-833-978-2511 (option 1). Source: EDD search result.
- **Automated line (24/7):** 1-866-333-4606 (payment info, Tele-Cert, phone application). Source: EDD search result.
- **TTY:** 1-800-815-9387. Relay 711. Source: EDD search result.
- **Certify by phone:** https://edd.ca.gov/en/unemployment/EDD_Tele-Cert/. Source: EDD search result.
- **In person:** EDD field offices. Locate at https://edd.ca.gov/en/about_edd/contact_edd/ (search result; contents not fetched). **UNCONFIRMED** for the field office list.

### Other states
- **CareerOneStop:** https://www.careeronestop.org (find unemployment benefits page). The URL was not confirmed (fetch returned 403). **UNCONFIRMED** for the exact page.
