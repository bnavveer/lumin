# BenefitsCal public form inventory and submission research

Researched 2026-10-11. This is a public-source preparation inventory, not a complete verification of the current live questionnaire. No account was accessed, no personal answers were entered, and no application or document was submitted for this research.

The [current CalSAWS BenefitsCal resource index](https://www.calsaws.org/benefitscal/) still links the 22-page Apply for Benefits guide at the 2021 URL below. Following that link on the research date returned the same guide. A filename date is not proof that every screen remains current. This guide walks through Your Information and then deliberately takes an early Review and Submit route; it names the middle sections but does not inventory their questions. Consult [the paper-form crosscheck](benefitscal-paper-crosscheck.md) for the broader SAWS 2 PLUS question inventory. Paper questions establish preparation topics, not exact live labels, controls, requiredness, or branch order.

## Whole application section map

The nine-section map is: Your Information → People → Household Details → Income → Expenses → Assets → Other Situations → Document Upload → Review and Submit. A p. 4 describes the first three sections unlocking in sequence; following Household Details, the applicant can visit income, expenses, assets, and other situations in a chosen order. A p. 16 shows People unlocking after Your Information and Household Details remaining locked until People is completed. W p. 5 identifies document upload as the eighth of nine parts. S p. 9 shows the nine-part sidebar, including an Assets section unavailable in the illustrated CalFresh-only example.

| Section | Public-guide coverage | Preparation/follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| Your Information | Detailed A pp. 5–15, plus college-student branch in S p. 5 | Inventory below. |
| People | Section named, no full regular-application fields in A | Use paper crosscheck for household roster, relationships, person-level program requests; current repeated-person screens remain unverified. |
| Household Details | Section named; S pp. 7–8 documents college/trade school and student exemption branching | Use paper crosscheck for disability/care, education, pregnancy, residence, foster care, tax household and related topics; UI controls remain unverified. |

| Other Situations | A names section, no exhaustive controls | Paper crosscheck covers the questions that may need preparation; current placement and branch triggers are unknown. |

## Entry, account and initial informational screens

A pp. 1–4 documents program support for CalFresh, CalWORKs, Medi-Cal, CMSP, GA/GR, and Disaster CalFresh; actual availability depends on county/program conditions. A p. 1 says only one application can be in progress. A p. 2 permits applying without logging in or creating an account and describes an account as useful for tracking. Starting controls include Apply Now, Getting Started information, expandable expectations/document/interview guidance, then Begin. Helpful tips and a diversity/equity/inclusion statement precede an optional feelings/sentiment choice. These information screens require navigation, not eligibility answers.

Account creation and case linking are separate flows. A p. 22 says selecting status tracking after guest submission directs the user to account creation. Email provided in the form is not equivalent to opting into electronic notices: A p. 11 explicitly describes electronic-notice consent as an account setting. Current authentication and account security controls are outside A's coverage; do not promise the 2021 sign-in experience.

## Documented student and disaster branches

S pp. 6–8: when CalFresh is selected and a person is identified as enrolled in college or trade school, student exemption questions follow in Household Details. This appears whether using student-tailored help or traditional help. The applicant should identify applicable situations; S's illustrated eligibility choices are historical, so confirm current exemptions rather than copying them as rules.

D p. 1: Disaster CalFresh is activated during a declared emergency at State/CDSS direction. Home and work location questions determine whether the applicant is affected before the disaster application path. Work inside a disaster area may matter even if the home is outside. This separate path is not evidence that Disaster CalFresh is always selectable.

R documents newer work-requirements screens. Its presence on the current index demonstrates that a 2021 questionnaire alone is insufficient for present-day program preparation. Work requirements and exemptions must use current official program material; they are not a universal prerequisite to pressing Submit.

Self-employment has its own bank/institution verification route (pp. 9–15). ## Document upload inventory and process

W p. 2: Document Upload shows document requests by case/individual, document example help, and upload actions on person/document tiles. Uploading no files produces a warning encouraging documents, not proof that submitting the application is blocked. A p. 16 permits upload or submission at that point. Do not delay starting an application solely because every proof is not ready; see P cover p. 2 for keeping an interview even with missing proof and asking county help.

U pp. 3–5 documents the signed-in route: choose **case/application number**, **person**, and **document type** (all required selections), choose files, check thumbnails/details, add another file if needed, then Upload. Receipt includes date/time, case/application number, person, files and category. Save by text/email/print; history contains the previous year of signed-in uploads and receipts, not accessible copies of the underlying files.

U pp. 6–12 documents the guest route: homepage upload entry → Begin → helpful tips → case/application number, county, first and last name, DOB, document type → optional notes → validation against existing application/case records → select files → confirm → Upload Document → save receipt. Mismatch requires correcting entered details. Guest uploads have no upload history, so saving the receipt matters. This supersedes the 2021 W/U statements that all later uploads require an account.

U pp. 3, 11 lists accepted extensions: GIF, GIFF, JPEG, JPG, JPE, JIF, MDI, BMP, PNG, TIF, TIFF, TXT, RTF, ONE, ONETOC, PDF, DOC, DOCX, DOX, PAGES, ODT, WPD, XLS, XLSX, PPT, PPTX, PSD, EPS, AI, LOG, WPS. Signed-in guidance says maximum 8 MB per file; guest guidance says smaller than 8 MB and no encryption/password protection. Safest preparation is a legible, unlocked PDF/JPG/PNG below 8 MB, while checking the actual current uploader's restrictions.

Files can be removed before upload; U p. 1 says uploaded files cannot be removed and go to CalSAWS. Preview carefully. Receipt success demonstrates transmission, not county approval or sufficiency of proof.

**Version conflict:** M printed p. 3 reports production deployment on August 7, 2025 of CSPM-80804, removing the combined Citizenship/Immigration/Birth Certificate category from Apply for Benefits, CalWORKs redetermination, SAR7 and CF37 flows. However, the October 11, 2026 [live walkthrough](benefitscal-live-walkthrough.md) found APDMC text explicitly offering that category for optional per-person uploads. The complete current category selector was not verified. Treat the report as historical evidence, not proof of current absence. Neither source establishes removal of citizenship questions or a universal exemption from verification.

## Preparation documents: program-specific, not a universal mandatory upload list

P cover p. 2 supplies older official examples for preparation: identity; residence; bank records; recent earned-income proof (past 30 days in that form), self-employment income/expense or tax records; unearned-income evidence; and applicable immigration proof for noncitizens applying. It separately identifies housing/phone/utilities, medical costs for elderly/disabled members, dependent-care costs and paid child support as evidence that can support CalFresh deductions. Additional health examples include current policy numbers and employer coverage; additional cash examples include applicants' birth certificates, young children's immunizations and vehicle registration. These are contextual examples from the 4/15 paper form, not current BenefitsCal required-upload categories. The county's actual requests, program rules, electronic verification and exemptions determine which proof is needed. See the paper crosscheck for details and limitations.

Prepare representative authorization/legal appointment documents if relevant, a Covered California case number if previously applied, and enough income/expense detail to answer emergency screening. Keep copies of what is sent. Never put actual SSNs, immigration identifiers, medical facts, passwords, or applicant documents into this public repository research.

## Remaining validation work

The public guide cannot establish a complete current form schema. Still unverified: every current program/county combination; all repeated household-person fields; requiredness and skip behavior; tax-filing/dependency and health-coverage branches; current student/exemption choices; full manual income controls; expense/asset/other-situation controls; emergency result thresholds; representative details; current notice consent; available document categories; all signature combinations; and error handling. A current live inventory needs representative branch exploration without invented personal answers or submission, or a current official schema supplied by BenefitsCal/CalSAWS. Keep public documentation, paper corroboration and live observation distinct in any application built from this research.
