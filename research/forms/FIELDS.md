# Canonical field list for comparing benefit applications

Use these exact keys. For each form, mark each key as:
- "Y" = the form asks for it
- "P" = partially (e.g. only for the applicant, not every household member, or only if a condition applies)
- "N" = not asked

## Identity
- full_name: legal name
- other_names: maiden/other names used
- dob: date of birth
- ssn: Social Security number
- sex_gender
- race_ethnicity: (usually optional)
- language: preferred language
- citizenship_status: citizen / immigrant status
- immigration_docs: A-number, document type/number
- photo_id: proof of identity required

## Contact
- home_address
- mailing_address
- phone
- email
- county

## Household
- household_members: names, DOB, relationship of each person
- members_ssn: SSN for each member
- members_income: income for each member
- pregnancy: pregnant / due date
- disability: disability or blind status
- student_status
- homeless_status: homeless or at risk
- foster_status
- veteran_status
- tribal_status

## Income
- job_income: employer, hours, pay rate, pay frequency, gross pay
- self_employment
- unemployment_benefits
- social_security_ssi: SSA/SSI/pension income
- child_support_received
- other_income: alimony, rental, gifts, etc.
- annual_income_estimate: projected yearly (tax-style) income
- prior_year_tax: last year's tax return info / AGI
- tax_filing_status: filing status and who claims whom
- employment_history: past employers and dates

## Assets
- bank_accounts: balances
- vehicles
- property_other_assets: real estate, investments, retirement

## Expenses
- rent_mortgage
- utilities_costs
- child_care_costs
- medical_expenses
- child_support_paid

## Health and insurance
- current_insurance
- employer_insurance_offer
- medicare_info

## Program-specific
- current_benefits: other programs you're on / case numbers
- utility_account_number
- landlord_info
- medical_providers: doctors, hospitals, conditions
- school_info: child's school, grade
- bank_direct_deposit: account/routing for payments
- signature_consent
- authorized_rep: someone applying on your behalf

## Proof documents
- proof_income: pay stubs, etc.
- proof_address
- proof_citizenship_immigration
- proof_expenses: rent receipts, bills
