## 1. Medicaid / Medi-Cal

**California**
- Online: BenefitsCal (state portal). Direct fetch returned 403, so the URL is **UNCONFIRMED**. County sites confirm BenefitsCal is the portal: https://www.shastacounty.gov/health-human-services/page/benefitscal
- Also apply through Covered California (one application screens for both Medi-Cal and Covered CA): https://www.coveredca.com/apply/
- Phone: DHCS Medi-Cal Helpline **(800) 541-5555**, free, Mon-Fri 8 a.m.-5 p.m. Outside CA: 1-916-636-1980. Source: https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/medi-cal/contact/ and https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/medi-cal/help/ (seen in search)
- County phone lines vary. Examples (seen in search, verify before calling):
  - San Diego: 1-866-262-9881 - https://www.sandiegocounty.gov/content/sdc/hhsa/programs/ssp/medi-cal_program/how_to_apply.html (**UNCONFIRMED** as current)
  - Contra Costa: (866) 663-3225 (**UNCONFIRMED**)
  - Los Angeles (DPSS): (866) 613-3777 - https://dpss.lacounty.gov/en/news/2020/03/customer_service.html (**UNCONFIRMED**, page is from 2020)
- In person: your county social services / human services office. Find it by searching "[your county] Human Services Medi-Cal" or through your county's official site. The state-level county directory URL was **UNCONFIRMED**.
- Paper form: the single streamlined application. Form number **UNCONFIRMED** (the 2014 DHCS flyer references the old form). Get it from your county office or BenefitsCal.
- Managed care plan choice: Health Care Options **1-800-430-4263 (UNCONFIRMED)**. Not searched.

**Other states**
- Official locator: https://www.healthcare.gov/ (find your state's Medicaid office) and https://www.medicaid.gov/ (state Medicaid contacts). Neither was fetched successfully; **UNCONFIRMED** for exact pages.

**What to bring:** ID, Social Security numbers (or immigration docs), proof of income for the past month (pay stubs, benefit letters), proof of residence, and any health insurance information.

**How long it takes**
- Decision deadline is 45 days from application; 90 days if disability or blindness must be determined. Source: https://stgenssa.sccgov.org/debs/program_handbooks/medi-cal/assets/05Apps/TimeframesProcApps.htm (seen in search)
- Retroactive coverage: up to 3 months before the application month for applications filed before 1/1/2027. Source: https://stgenssa.sccgov.org/debs/program_handbooks/medi-cal/assets/05Apps/RetroactiveMC.htm
- Changes for applications filed on or after 1/1/2027 (federal H.R. 1): retro limited to 1 month for new adult groups, 2 months for others. Source: https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/ACWDL-26-07.pdf (seen in search)

**Tip:** Hospital Presumptive Eligibility gives temporary coverage at the hospital the same day, based on self-attestation. Source: https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/services/medi-cal-resources/medi-cal-eligibility-division/hospital-presumptive-eligibility-program/ (seen in search)

## 2. Medi-Cal for Pregnancy, MCAP, and Presumptive Eligibility

**California**
- Presumptive Eligibility for Pregnant People (PE4PP): apply at a qualified provider (clinic or doctor). Provides temporary outpatient prenatal coverage. Source: https://mcweb.apps.prd.cammis.medi-cal.ca.gov/references/pe4pp (seen in search)
- Hospital PE (pregnancy version): limited to outpatient prenatal care, outpatient abortion, and pregnancy-related drugs. Same source as Section 1 tip.
- Medi-Cal Access Program (MCAP): for uninsured pregnant people with income about 213%-322% FPL (seen in search). No separate form; apply through Covered California online at https://www.coveredca.com/apply/ or by phone (800) 300-1506. Source for MCAP rules: https://stgenssa.sccgov.org/debs/program_handbooks/medi-cal/assets/35StCtyAdminHealthIns/MCAccessProgFormrlyAIM.htm (seen in search; 2020s county handbook, **verify**).
- Phone for PE questions: Medi-Cal line (800) 541-5555 (see Section 1).
- Paper form: same single application as Section 1, or the Covered CA paper application (mail to P.O. Box 989725, West Sacramento, CA 95798-9725). Source: https://www.coveredca.com/apply/

**Other states:** Use the same Medicaid locator as Section 1. Many states have a separate pregnancy Medicaid program; check with your state Medicaid office.

**What to bring:** Same as Medi-Cal, plus proof of pregnancy if your provider asks for it.

**How long it takes:** PE is effective immediately. Full determination follows the 45-day rule above. If you are found eligible after the PE period, you may get 12 months of postpartum coverage. Source: https://stgenssa.sccgov.org/debs/program_handbooks/medi-cal/assets/35StCtyAdminHealthIns/PresumpEligPregnantInd.htm (seen in search)

**Tip:** Ask your provider about PE at your first prenatal visit. PE can start coverage the same day.

## 3. CHIP / Kids' Coverage

**California:** Kids are enrolled in Medi-Cal through the same application as Section 1 (BenefitsCal, county office, or Covered CA). Phone: Medi-Cal Helpline (800) 541-5555.

**Other states**
- Official site: https://www.insurekidsnow.gov (fetched successfully)
- Phone: **1-877-KIDS-NOW (1-877-543-7669)**. Source: https://www.insurekidsnow.gov
- State locator: https://www.insurekidsnow.gov/coverage (the "View State" link). Choose your state there.
- Paper form: the state's CHIP/Medicaid application, available from your state program.

**What to bring:** Child's birth certificate or ID, proof of income, proof of residence, and the child's SSN if they have one.

**How long it takes:** Same 45-day standard as Medi-Cal. Retroactive coverage applies (see Section 1).

**Tip:** Medi-Cal and CHIP do not have an asset test for children in California, so a child can qualify even if the parent's income is above the adult limit.
Verify this before relying on it (**UNCONFIRMED**).

## 4. ACA Marketplace / Covered California

**California**
- Online: https://apply.coveredca.com (application entry point, seen on https://www.coveredca.com/apply/)
- Phone: **(800) 300-1506**. Source: https://www.coveredca.com/apply/
- In person: certified enroller or licensed agent. Find one at https://www.coveredca.com/get-help/find-an-enroller (seen on coveredca.com/apply)
- Paper form: available in 12 languages. Mail to Covered California, P.O. Box 989725, West Sacramento, CA 95798-9725. Source: https://www.coveredca.com/apply/

**Other states**
- Official site: https://www.healthcare.gov
- Phone: **1-800-318-2596** (TTY 1-855-889-4325). Seen in search results; **verify on https://www.healthcare.gov/contact-us/ (UNCONFIRMED)**.
- Local help finder: https://localhelp.healthcare.gov (seen in search)

**Open enrollment dates**
- California (for 2027 coverage): Nov 1 - Jan 31. Source: https://www.coveredca.com/apply/
- Federal (HealthCare.gov): **UNCONFIRMED**. The official page returned 404 when fetched.

**Special enrollment (year-round):** losing coverage, marriage, birth or adoption, moving, or change in immigration status. Source: https://www.coveredca.com/apply/

**What to bring:** SSNs, income for the past month or the year (tax return or W-2), employer coverage info, household size, and ID.

**How long it takes:** Coverage typically starts the first of the month after you pick a plan (**UNCONFIRMED**, not checked).

**Tip:** One Covered CA application also screens you for Medi-Cal and MCAP. Start there if you are unsure which you qualify for.

## 5. Medicare Savings Programs (QMB / SLMB / QI)

**California**
- Apply with Medi-Cal and MSP together through BenefitsCal or your county social services office. Source: https://cahealthadvocates.org/low-income-help/medicare-savings-programs-msps/ (seen in search)
- Paper form (MSP-only): MC 14A. Source: https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/MC14A-Eng-1.pdf (seen in search)
- Phone (free counseling): HICAP **800-434-0222**. Source: https://www.aging.ca.gov/Programs_and_Services/Medicare_Counseling/ (seen in search; page not fetched)
- In person: local HICAP offices. Find one by county at https://www.aging.ca.gov/Programs_and_Services/Medicare_Counseling/ (seen in search)
- Income limits vary by source (2026 QMB single: $1,330 vs. $1,350 in different sources). Confirm with DHCS or BenefitsCal.

**Other states**
- SHIP locator: https://www.shiphelp.org (fetched). Pick your state from the list. National SHIP TA line: 877-839-2675.
- State Medicaid office: https://www.medicaid.gov/ (see Section 1).

**What to bring:** Medicare card, proof of income for the past month, bank statements, ID, and proof of residence.

**How long it takes:** About 45 days (same Medi-Cal rule; **UNCONFIRMED** for MSP specifically).

**Tip:** If you do not qualify for full Medi-Cal, QI still covers the Part B premium. Ask HICAP to screen you.

## 6. Extra Help (Medicare Part D Low-Income Subsidy)

- Online: https://www.ssa.gov/medicare/part-d-extra-help (seen in search; ssa.gov fetch returned 403)
- Phone: **1-800-772-1213**. TTY **1-800-325-0778**. Source: https://www.ssa.gov/medicare/part-d-extra-help (seen in search)
- In person: Social Security office. Find your nearest one at https://www.ssa.gov/locator (**UNCONFIRMED**, not fetched).
- Paper form: SSA-1020 (request by phone). Source: https://www.ssa.gov/medicare/part-d-extra-help (seen in search)
- Automatic eligibility: people already on Medi-Cal, SSI, or an MSP generally do not need to apply. Source: https://www.ssa.gov/pubs/EN-05-10508.pdf (seen in search)

**What to bring:** SSN, bank statements, tax returns, and statements for pensions or retirement accounts (you and your spouse).

**How long it takes:** Processing time **UNCONFIRMED**. You can apply before or after enrolling in Part D.

**Tip:** Apply even if your drug costs seem low. Extra Help also lowers premiums and copays for later years.

## 7. IHSS (In-Home Supportive Services) and Other States' Home Care

**California**
- Program info: https://www.cdss.ca.gov/in-home-supportive-services (seen in search)
- Form: SOC 295 (Application for IHSS). Submit to your county IHSS office. Some counties also accept phone applications. Examples: Napa County, https://www.napacounty.gov/3799/Apply-For-IHSS (seen in search); Solano County, https://www.solanocounty.gov/government/health-social-services-hss/older-disabled-adult-services/home-supportive-services (seen in search).
- Phone: find your county IHSS office by searching "[county] IHSS" on your county's official site. The IHSS help center number was **UNCONFIRMED**.
- In person: county IHSS office.

**Other states**
- Eldercare Locator: https://eldercare.acl.gov or **1-800-677-1116**. Source: https://eldercare.acl.gov/home (seen in search)
- Look for your Area Agency on Aging (AAA), Medicaid home- and community-based services (HCBS) waivers, or your state Medicaid office.

**What to bring:** SSN, Medi-Cal or Medicaid card or application number, proof of income, and a Health Care Certification form (required by the state for IHSS). Source: https://www.napacounty.gov/3799/Apply-For-IHSS (seen in search)

**How long it takes:** Up to 90 days, including eligibility, in-home assessment, and service authorization. Source: https://www.solanocounty.gov/government/health-social-services-hss/older-disabled-adult-services/home-supportive-services (seen in search)

**Tip:** IHSS requires Medi-Cal eligibility. Apply for Medi-Cal first or at the same time.

## 8. Family PACT (Free Family Planning, California)

- Find a provider: https://familypact.org/provider-search/ (fetched)
- Apply (optional): pre-application survey at https://apply.fpact.dhcs.ca.gov/static/lw-prescreener and the application at https://apply.fpact.dhcs.ca.gov/static/lw-account/account-creation. Source: https://www.familypact.org (fetched)
- Phone: **UNCONFIRMED**. None listed on the fetched page.
- In person: the provider you choose. No prior application is needed to get services. Source: https://www.familypact.org (fetched; the page suggests applying but does not say you must apply first)

**Other states:** Title X clinic locator at https://opa-fpclinicdb.hhs.gov (HHS Office of Population Affairs). Source: https://opa-fpclinicdb.hhs.gov/about-this-tool (seen in search). Also check your state Medicaid family-planning program.

**What to bring:** Nothing required. ID and insurance card if you have them.

**How long it takes:** Services are typically same day. Title X clinics offer care regardless of ability to pay. Source: https://opa-fpclinicdb.hhs.gov/about-this-tool (seen in search)

**Tip:** Patients under 100% FPL pay nothing at Title X clinics. Source: same URL (seen in search).

## 9. Free / Low-Cost Clinics

**All states**
- HRSA Find a Health Center: https://findahealthcenter.hrsa.gov (URL confirmed; page content did not load in full, so the search function is **UNCONFIRMED**).

**California (county programs)**
- Los Angeles County DHS Ability to Pay Program (ATP): patients at or under 200% FPL get care at no cost; higher incomes pay a sliding-scale fee. Source: https://dhs.lacounty.gov/patient-information/get-coverage/financial-services/ (seen in search). Fee figures in other sources are outdated; confirm with DHS.
- Other counties: search "[county] public health clinic sliding scale" or call 211.

**What to bring:** Proof of income (or a statement of no income), household size, ID, and any insurance card.

**How long it takes:** Often same day for clinic visits. Eligibility for county programs (ATP) can take a few weeks (**UNCONFIRMED**).

**Tip:** Ask about a sliding-scale fee before your visit. Many clinics charge little or nothing for patients under 200% FPL.
