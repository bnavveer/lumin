# Federal benefits: food, cash, health (2026 / FY2027)

Researched 2026-10-11. Figures are for the 48 contiguous states + D.C. Status tags: CONFIRMED = official source; SECONDARY = third-party only; UNCONFIRMED = not verified.

## Baseline
- 2026 HHS poverty guideline (FPL): **$15,960** for 1 person, **+$5,680** per extra person. CONFIRMED. https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/01/15/2026-00755/annual-update-of-the-hhs-poverty-guidelines
- 2025 FPL (used for 2026 ACA subsidies): $15,650, +$5,500 per person.

## SNAP
- FY2027 (Oct 1, 2026 – Sep 30, 2027): gross limit 130% FPL ($1,729/mo for 1; $3,575/mo for 4); net limit 100% ($1,330/mo for 1). https://www.fns.usda.gov/snap/income-eligibility-standards
- Max allotment: $306 (1), $562 (2), $1,023 (4); minimum benefit $25. https://www.usda.gov/sites/default/files/guidance-documents/fna.snap-cola2027.pdf
- Asset limits: $3,000 general, $4,750 with elderly/disabled member (re-verify). Broad-based categorical eligibility waives the asset test in most states (state count UNCONFIRMED).
- Households with an elderly/disabled member only need to pass the net-income test.
- 2025 law (OBBBA) work rules: ABAWD time limit now ages 18–64; parent exemption only for a child under 14; veteran / homeless / former-foster-youth exemptions removed; tribal members newly exempt. States enforced from late 2025 to March 2026 as waivers ended. https://www.legalservicesnyc.org/resources/do-changes-to-work-requirements-for-snap-benefits-affect-me-do-i-need-to-do-anything/
- Non-citizens (OBBBA Sec. 10108): limited to citizens, certain LPRs (generally after a 5-year bar), Cuban/Haitian entrants, and COFA citizens. Refugees, asylees and others lost eligibility. USDA memo Oct 31, 2025, being litigated. SECONDARY: https://www.globalrefuge.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Updated-GR-OBBBA-FAQ-on-SNAP.pdf

## WIC
- 185% FPL: $29,526/yr for 1, +$10,508 per person; July 2026 – June 2027. CONFIRMED. https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/04/29/2026-08323/special-supplemental-nutrition-program-for-women-infants-and-children-wic-20262027-income
- Pregnant/postpartum/breastfeeding people, infants, children under 5. Adjunctive eligibility through SNAP, Medicaid or TANF.

## School meals and Summer EBT
- Free ≤130% FPL; reduced ≤185% (lunch ≤40¢). Effective July 1, 2026 – June 30, 2027. https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/04/09/2026-06842/child-nutrition-programs-income-eligibility-guidelines
- Direct certification for SNAP/TANF/FDPIR households. Community Eligibility Provision lets high-poverty schools feed everyone free.
- Summer EBT (SUN Bucks): $120 per child per summer. About 12 states don't participate (list UNCONFIRMED). SECONDARY: https://feedam.org/summer-ebt-2026

## TANF
- 60-month federal lifetime limit; hardship exemptions capped at 20% of caseload. https://www.acf.hhs.gov/ofa/faq/q-time-limits
- Benefit levels set by states and often very low (2021: 18 states paid ≤$362/mo for a family of 3). 2026 ranges UNCONFIRMED.

## SSI
- 2026: $994/mo individual, $1,491 couple (2.8% COLA). Resources: $2,000 / $3,000. CONFIRMED. https://www.ssa.gov/oact/cola/SSI.html
- For people 65+, blind or disabled. Leads to Medicaid in most states, plus SNAP, Extra Help and MSPs.

## Medicaid
- Expansion adults: 138% FPL (≈$22,025 for 1 person, computed).
- 10 non-expansion states: AL, FL, GA, KS, MS, SC, TN, TX, WI, WY. https://www.kff.org/affordable-care-act/state-indicator/state-activity-around-expanding-medicaid-under-the-affordable-care-act/
- OBBBA community engagement requirement: expansion adults 19–64 need 80 hrs/mo of work, school, service or training, or ≥$580/mo income. States must implement by **Jan 1, 2027**. CMS interim final rule CMS-2454-IFC, June 2026. https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/medicaid-community-engagement-requirement-certain-individuals-interim-final-rule-comment-period-cms

## CHIP
- Kids' coverage, about 170%–400% FPL depending on state. Exact limits UNCONFIRMED.

## Medicare Savings Programs and Extra Help (2026)
- Monthly income limits, individual / couple: QMB $1,350 / $1,824; SLMB $1,616 / $2,184; QI $1,816 / $2,455. Resources $9,950 / $14,910. https://www.medicare.gov/medicare-savings-programs
- Extra Help: income $23,940 / $32,460; resources $18,090 / $36,100. https://www.medicare.gov/basics/costs/help/drug-costs

## ACA marketplace
- Enhanced subsidies expired Dec 31, 2025, so the 400% FPL "cliff" is back for 2026. https://www.kff.org/affordable-care-act/what-we-know-so-far-about-2026-aca-marketplace-enrollment-premiums-and-deductibles/

## Tax credits
- EITC, tax year 2026 (Rev. Proc. 2025-32, SECONDARY): $664 (no kids), $4,427 (1 child), $7,316 (2), $8,231 (3+). Tax year 2025 (IRS): $649 / $4,328 / $7,152 / $8,046. https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/individuals/earned-income-tax-credit/earned-income-and-earned-income-tax-credit-eitc-tables
- Child Tax Credit (OBBBA): $2,200 per child, refundable up to $1,700. SSNs required. SECONDARY.

## Senior food / food banks
- CSFP: age 60+, ≤130% FPL federally (some states use 150%). https://www.fns.usda.gov/csfp/applicant-recipient
- TEFAP: no federal asset test; states set income limits (often ~185%). https://www.fns.usda.gov/tefap
