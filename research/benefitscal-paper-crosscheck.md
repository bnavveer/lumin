# SAWS 2 PLUS paper application crosscheck

Source: [SAWS 2 PLUS (4/15)](https://www.cdss.ca.gov/cdssweb/entres/forms/English/SAWS2_PLUS.pdf) (29 PDF pages). Retrieved 2026-10-11.

This catalog is a historical **paper reference, not the current live BenefitsCal schema**. Every record is marked `paper_reference_not_live`. Stable keys are analyst-created, and input labels below are compact paper-derived summaries. The form is dated April 2015; its policy thresholds, legal wording, services, and route instructions need current verification before use. No real applicant information was used and no application was submitted.

Page references below use printed application pages (1–17); physical PDF page = application page + 6. Appendices A/B/C/D-1/D-2/E are physical PDF pages 24/25/26/27/28/29.

## Explicit filing, applicability, and optionality instructions

- Coversheet PDF p1: CalFresh application can begin with name, address, and signature. Cash-aid process can begin with Q1–5 and signature. General Assistance/General Relief questions depend on County instructions. These are minimum filing statements, not a claim that eligibility can be decided with only those inputs.
- Coversheet PDF p1: fill entire form if possible; incomplete questions may be finished at interview; answer questions carrying selected-program symbols. This inventory does not exhaustively assign those image symbols.
- Program Rules PDF p4: noncitizen family members not applying need not provide immigration information, SSNs, or documents; income/resource information still requested. Current legal-policy verification is outside this crosscheck.
- Q3 race/ethnicity is optional. Q6a email is optional. Q6/6b SSNs are optional for members not applying; citizenship question only people applying.
- Q24 explicitly requires answer for cash aid and CalFresh; healthcare resource questions Q24–27 and Appendix E are described as optional and only answered for applicants 65+ or disabled. Q26 and Appendix E explicitly require cash-aid answers. These are 2015 paper instructions, not current policy.
- Q15 says actual heating/cooling, phone, other-utility, and shelter amounts need not be provided. Q38 says answers do not affect eligibility.
- Applicability and requiredness of all other inputs below are **not inferred**. Conditional detail instructions are recorded separately.

## Non-input pages and supporting proof

Application p17 (physical PDF p23) is additional writing space and **County use only** expedited-processing questions. It is excluded from applicant inputs. Additional sheets may be used, identifying the relevant question; Appendix D and other repeatable sections may be copied as instructed. Program Rules are acknowledgments incorporated into Q1 signature, not extra invented input fields.

Coversheet PDF p2 lists proofs to bring to the interview and says to keep the interview even without proof; County may help obtain proof. These are not asserted as mandatory upload requirements at online submission: identification; cash-aid applicants birth certificates; residence proof; applicable applicant SSNs; household bank statements; earned income past 30 days (self-employment income/expense or tax records); unearned income; lawful immigration status for applicable noncitizen applicants, with noted exceptions; housing/utilities; elderly/disabled medical costs; care expenses for work/job-search/training/school/required activity; child support paid; job health-insurance information/current policy numbers; cash-aid child immunizations age six or younger and vehicle registration. Appendix C asks proof for legally appointed representatives; Appendix E specifies DMV proof on gift/donation/family-transfer stop path.
