# California Low-Income Health Programs: Research Notes (2026)

**Date:** 2026-10-11
**Scope:** California state health programs for low-income residents: Medi-Cal (adult, child, pregnancy, aged/disabled, immigrant rules), Medicare Savings Programs, Covered California, IHSS, Family PACT, Medi-Cal Dental, CalAIM Community Supports, and county indigent care (My Health LA).
**Baseline:** 2026 federal poverty level (FPL) = $15,960 for 1 person, +$5,680 per additional person.

## Method and caveats

- Facts come from web search and WebFetch. Several official pages (DHCS I25-23, DHCS immigrant FAQ, DHCS 26-01, DHCS work requirements page, CDSS IHSS page) returned empty content or 404 on fetch. Where a fact is taken from a search snippet that quotes an official page, it is labeled **(snippet)**. Verify those against the live page before relying on them.
- Dollar amounts marked **(derived)** were computed from the 2026 FPL in the brief, then cross-checked against secondary sources.
- **UNCONFIRMED** means no source was found that confirmed the item.
- Phone numbers and URLs are listed only where a source was found. Check them before publishing.

## 1. Medi-Cal (general, adult, children, pregnancy)

**Who**
- Adults 19–64 (expansion group), up to **138% FPL**: **$22,025/yr for 1 person (~$1,836/mo)** (derived; matches the 2026 figure from a secondary source citing DHCS ACWDL 26-01). Family of 4: $45,540/yr.
  - Source: https://www.coverhealthca.com/learn/medi-cal-income-limits (secondary; cites DHCS)
  - Official: https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/es/services/medi-cal/eligibility/letters/Documents/26-01.pdf (letter was not readable on fetch)
- Children 0–18, up to **266% FPL**: **~$42,454/yr for 1 person (derived)**. Family of 4: $87,780/yr.
  - Source: https://www.coverhealthca.com/learn/medi-cal-income-limits (secondary)
- Pregnancy, full-scope up to **213% FPL**: **~$33,995/yr for 1 person (derived)**. Family of 4: $70,290/yr.
  - MCAP (Medi-Cal Access Program) covers pregnancy-related care from 213% to **322% FPL** (~$51,391/yr for 1 person, derived) with a small monthly premium.
  - Source: https://www.coverhealthca.com/learn/medi-cal-income-limits (secondary)
- Note: one site lists the adult 1-person limit as $21,597. That figure is 138% of the 2025 FPL and is outdated.
- **Immigration status:** Full-scope eligibility depends on federal immigration status. Undocumented adults are covered under state-funded rules, subject to the 2026 freeze and changes listed below. Children and pregnant people are exempt from the freeze.
  - Source: https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/medi-cal-immigrant-eligibility-faqs/ (snippet)

**Cost**
- No premium for most full-scope enrollees. MCAP pregnancy coverage has a small monthly premium (amount **UNCONFIRMED**).
- Undocumented adults: see the $30 premium below.

**Changes (2025–2026)**
- **Asset test reinstated Jan 1, 2026.** Limit **$130,000 for 1 person, +$65,000 for each additional household member, up to 10 people**. Applies to applicants and members who are 65+, disabled, in a nursing home, or in a family over the income limit under federal tax rules.
  - Source: https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/hm/services/medi-cal/eligibility/letters/Documents/I25-23.pdf (snippet; fetch failed). Secondary: https://healthlaw.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025.08.20-Medi-Cal-Fact-Sheet-Home-Equity-and-Asset-Limits-FINAL.pdf
  - Primary home and one vehicle are generally excluded; secondary homes and vehicles count (per DHCS snippet). The exact vehicle rules are **UNCONFIRMED**.
  - Couple limit **$195,000** (derived from the $65,000 add-on; secondary sources agree). **UNCONFIRMED** as a DHCS-stated couple figure.
  - Look-back for transfers: one secondary source says 30 months, phasing in. **UNCONFIRMED.**
- **Enrollment freeze, Jan 1, 2026.** No new Medi-Cal enrollment for adults 19+ with unsatisfactory immigration status (UIS). Existing enrollees keep coverage if they renew on time. Children and pregnant people are exempt.
  - Source: https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/medi-cal-immigrant-eligibility-faqs/ (snippet); https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/tracking-federal-impact-medi-cal-eligibility/ (snippet)
- **Dental cut, July 1, 2026.** Routine dental ends for UIS adults 19+. Emergency dental stays. Date conflict: the DHCS Help Center snippet says July 1, 2027. DHCS dental notices say July 1, 2026. Treat July 1, 2026 as the operative date until the live DHCS notice is checked.
  - Source: https://dental.dhcs.ca.gov/Providers/Medi_Cal_Dental/BenefitChanges (snippet)
- **$30 monthly premium for UIS adults.** Ages 19–59; pregnant people and adults 60+ exempt. Start **July 1, 2027**.
  - Source: https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/tracking-federal-impact-medi-cal-eligibility/ (snippet). Other sources say "after Jan 1, 2027," which conflicts. The DHCS page supports July 1, 2027.
  - A May 2026 revision proposed raising this to $50. **Proposal only, not enacted.**
  - After a 90-day grace period for nonpayment, coverage moves to emergency-only. Three consecutive missed payments lead to disenrollment.
- **Federal H.R. 1 changes:**
  - Oct 1, 2026: federal full-scope eligibility narrows for certain immigration statuses. Affected members move to state-funded full-scope until June 30, 2027.
  - Jan 1, 2027: UIS members must receive care through Medi-Cal fee-for-service, not managed care.
  - Source: https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/tracking-federal-impact-medi-cal-eligibility/ (snippet)
- **Work requirement, Jan 1, 2027 (federal H.R. 1, implemented in CA):**
  - Applies to expansion adults ages 19–64. Requires **80 hours/month** of work, community service, work program, or at least half-time school. Seasonal work averaged over six months.
  - Proof due at application and each redetermination. 30 days to comply or qualify for an exemption before coverage ends.
  - Exemptions include parents/caregivers of a child 13 or younger or disabled, pregnant and postpartum people, foster youth, American Indians/Alaska Natives, medically frail people, and a short-term hardship exemption.
  - Source: https://www.cencalhealth.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/26-018_2027-Medi-Cal-FSA-Changes_R10-1.pdf (secondary); https://dhs.saccounty.gov/PRI/Documents/SCHA/2025/CPC/12.2025/HR%201%20Medi-Cal%20Impacts_for%20Presentations.pdf (secondary)
  - Official: DHCS ACL 26-30 and Information Letter I 26-25 (cited in secondary sources; not fetched). **Details still preliminary.**
  - One state Assembly analysis projects up to 233K members losing coverage by June 2027. Estimate only.
- **Six-month renewals, Jan 1, 2027:** expansion adults are redetermined every 6 months instead of annually. Children, pregnant/postpartum members, and tribal members are excluded. Retroactive coverage for the adult expansion group shrinks to one month.
  - Source: same secondary sources as above.

**How to apply**
- Online: **https://benefitscal.com** (BenefitsCal). Also through Covered California at **https://www.coveredca.com** (one application can check both).
- Local office: BenefitsCal "Find an Office" tool (enter county and ZIP). Source: https://benefitscal.com/Help/find-an-office/HCCOL?lang=en
- Phone: **844-859-2100** appears on a BenefitsCal page, tied to text messages about a case. Confirm it is the general line. Source: BenefitsCal search result. **UNCONFIRMED** as the general Medi-Cal line.
- Medi-Cal does not require an interview at application; the county schedules one if needed (source: BenefitsCal FAQ search result).

## 2. Medi-Cal for seniors and people with disabilities (Aged & Disabled, Medicare Savings Programs)

**Who**
- **Aged & Disabled (ABD) Medi-Cal income limit for 2026: UNCONFIRMED.**
  - $1,836/mo is the adult 138% MAGI figure, not the ABD limit (search result).
  - One secondary source says the ABD non-MAGI framework uses SSI Federal Benefit Rate: **$994/mo individual, $1,491/mo couple** (2026). **UNCONFIRMED** against DHCS.
  - Another source says DHCS will publish 2026 ABD figures in an All County Letter. Not found.
- **Asset limit reinstated Jan 1, 2026:** $130,000 individual / $195,000 couple. Secondary sources; see Medi-Cal section.
- **Medicare Savings Programs (MSP), 2026 California, per a county benefits chart (secondary):**
  - QMB (Qualified Medicare Beneficiary): income at or below 100% FPL = $1,330/mo (derived), with $20 disregard = $1,350.
  - SLMB (Specified Low-Income Medicare Beneficiary): below 120% FPL = $1,596/mo (derived), $1,616 with disregard.
  - QI (Qualifying Individual): at or below 135% FPL = $1,795.50/mo (derived), $1,815.50 with disregard.
  - Asset limit: $130,000 individual / $195,000 couple (same county chart).
  - Source: county benefits chart cited in search result (sccgov-hosted chart, secondary). Verify against official DHCS.
  - Also: https://www.medicareresources.org/states/california-guide-content/ (secondary)

**Cost**
- MSP pays Medicare Part B premiums (and cost sharing for QMB). Medi-Cal share of cost for ABD: **UNCONFIRMED** (not researched).

**Changes (2025–2026)**
- Asset limit reinstated Jan 1, 2026 (see above).

**How to apply**
- Same as Medi-Cal: **https://benefitscal.com**, or the county office.
- MSP: apply through the county or via BenefitsCal. Confirm the MSP pathway on the official DHCS site (not fetched).

## 3. Covered California (state premium subsidies and cost-sharing reductions)

**Who**
- The state subsidy covers households up to **165% FPL**. Up to 150% FPL, it keeps IRA-level subsidy levels. Between 150% and 165% FPL, it lowers the premiums that federal rules require.
  - Source: https://www.coveredca.com/newsroom/news-releases/2026/02/26/as-enhanced-federal-subsidies-expire-covered-california-ends-open-enrollment-with-state-subsidies-keeping-renewals-steady-for-now-and-new-signups-down/ (fetched, confirmed)
- 165% FPL for 1 person = **$26,334/yr (derived)**. 150% = $23,940/yr (derived).
- The release lists an individual threshold of **$23,475**. That figure equals 150% of the 2025 FPL, not 2026. Treat it as **UNCONFIRMED** for 2026.

**Cost**
- $190 million state allocation (HCARF). About 389,590 enrollees; average **$45/month** in assistance (2026).
- Premiums rose more than 10% on average for 2026 (PPIC; source: https://www.ppic.org/?p=95805, secondary). Another group reports a much larger figure; the two measures differ.

**Changes (2025–2026)**
- Federal enhanced premium tax credits expired at the end of 2025. Congress has not restored them.
- State subsidies partially replace them. Covered California says the state allocation does not come close to the $2.5 billion in federal enhanced subsidies Californians received in 2025.
- Middle-income renewing enrollees who lost all enhanced credits had a 22% cancellation rate, double last year's 11%.
- **Cost-sharing reductions (CSR): UNCONFIRMED.** Covered California's release does not mention CSRs. A federal change reportedly ends PTC and CSR eligibility for lawfully present immigrants starting Jan 1, 2027 (search snippet from a secondary source; **UNCONFIRMED**).

**Open enrollment for 2027 coverage**
- **Nov 1, 2026 – Jan 31, 2027.** Enroll by **Dec 31, 2026** for Jan 1, 2027 coverage.
- Renewals and plan changes for current enrollees open **Oct 1, 2026**.
- Source: https://www.coveredca.com/newsroom/news-releases/2026/08/25/covered-california-announces-premium-change-for-2027-dental-plans-while-continuing-to-deliver-affordable-coverage-options/ (search snippet; press release dated Aug 25, 2026). Window confirmed by CP briefings (search snippet).

**How to apply**
- Online: **https://www.coveredca.com**
- Phone: **(800) 300-1506**, Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–6 p.m. Verified on https://www.coveredca.com/get-started/ (fetched).
- If you may qualify for Medi-Cal, apply on BenefitsCal or contact the county (per the same page).

## 4. IHSS (In-Home Supportive Services)

**Who**
- California resident with a Medi-Cal eligibility determination.
- Lives at home (not a hospital, long-term care, or licensed community care facility).
- Licensed health professional must certify the person cannot independently perform one or more activities of daily living, and the condition lasts at least 12 consecutive months or is expected to result in death within 12 months (Health Care Certification form).
- Income: IHSS runs through Medi-Cal, so Medi-Cal income and asset rules apply. Specific IHSS income cutoffs were not confirmed.
- Source: https://www.cdss.ca.gov/in-home-supportive-services (snippet; CDSS page fetch returned 404) and https://www.cdss.ca.gov/cdssweb/entres/forms/english/soc295.pdf (application form)

**Cost**
- No premium. Recipients hire their own individual provider.

**Changes (2025–2026)**
- May Revision proposed capping IHSS provider overtime at 50 hours/week. **Dropped** in the enacted 2025–26 budget. The existing 66-hour limit (90 with exemption) remains.
- Proposal to eliminate IHSS for undocumented adults (est. $158.8M savings in 2025–26) was **not adopted** in the final 2025–26 budget.
- The Medi-Cal freeze did pass. Because IHSS runs through Medi-Cal, new UIS enrollees cannot get IHSS through Medi-Cal. Existing enrollees keep it if they renew on time.
- Source for the budget items: search snippets from SCAN Foundation, Disability Rights CA, and Coalition CCC (secondary). **Verify** against enacted 2025–26 budget language. 2026–27 IHSS proposals: **UNCONFIRMED**.

**How to apply**
- Complete form SOC 295 and submit it to your **county IHSS office**. Find the county office at https://www.cdss.ca.gov/in-home-supportive-services/ihss-help-center (search result).
- A county social worker interviews you at home. The county sends a Notice of Action.
- Phone numbers: **UNCONFIRMED** (not found; use the county IHSS office listing).

## 5. Smaller programs (one or two lines each)

**Family PACT (free family planning)**
- Who: California residents with gross household income at or below **200% FPL**: **$31,920 for 1 person (derived)**. Minors apply on their own; parental income is not counted. Generally no other coverage that includes family planning.
- Cost: free.
- How to apply: at a participating Family PACT provider (Planned Parenthood, campus health centers, clinics). Not through the state. Approved people get a HAP card.
- Source: search results (secondary) plus DHCS I 26-16 (https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/vi/services/medi-cal/eligibility/letters/Documents/I26-16.pdf). Immigration status rules: **UNCONFIRMED**. Phone: **UNCONFIRMED**.

**CHDP (Child Health and Disability Prevention) replacement**
- **UNCONFIRMED.** Not researched. Check the DHCS site before citing.

**Medi-Cal Dental / Denti-Cal**
- Adult routine dental ends **July 1, 2026** for UIS adults 19+ (see Medi-Cal section). Emergency dental care remains. Pregnant people (through 1 year postpartum), people under 19, and former foster youth under 26 keep full dental.
- Phone: **(800) 423-0507**, Mon–Fri 8 a.m.–5 p.m. (Medi-Cal Dental Telephone Service Center). Source: https://dental.dhcs.ca.gov/Providers/Medi_Cal_Dental/BenefitChanges (snippet).
- How to apply: through BenefitsCal or county. Denti-Cal enrollment steps: **UNCONFIRMED**.

**CalAIM Community Supports (through Medi-Cal managed care)**
- Offered by managed care plans in all 58 counties. Availability varies by plan.
- Medically Tailored Meals / Medically Supportive Food: the most-used benefit in 2025, serving 156,000+ members. Election varies by plan (June 2026 DHCS election chart).
- Housing Transition/Navigation: a standard benefit.
- Short-Term Post-Hospitalization Housing ends Dec 31, 2026.
- Source: https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/file/community-supports-elections-by-mcp-and-county/ (snippet; June 2026 version)
- How to apply: ask your Medi-Cal managed care plan or county.

## 6. County indigent care: My Health LA (Los Angeles County)

- **Status: ended.** My Health LA ended **Jan 31, 2024**. The county is no longer enrolling people. Uninsured residents are directed to full-scope Medi-Cal.
- Source: https://dhs.lacounty.gov/my-health-la/?p=156 (search snippet)
- Where to go now: Medi-Cal via https://benefitscal.com or your medical home clinic.
- LA County Office of Immigrant Affairs (questions on public charge): **(800) 593-8222**. Source: same DHS page (snippet).
- Other counties: **UNCONFIRMED** (not researched).
