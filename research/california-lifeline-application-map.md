## Enrollment sequence and boundaries

1. Select/contact a participating phone or broadband provider. Official FAQ says the provider starts the application and may help complete it immediately; otherwise the Administrator sends the pink packet. Public CPUC overview also points applicants to participating providers. No current public self-initiation form was established.
2. Review the provider-supplied applicant name, principal home address, mailing address, and LifeLine service phone. Sample p7 explicitly says do not correct on the application; contact provider within 30 days to correct. Keep actual issued response date. Official FAQ describes 45 days; issued deadline governs workflow.
3. Choose paper or online completion. Online Customer Portal is **email username + user-created password + MFA**, not PIN. Official portal help dates PIN removal to December15,2023. MFA supports text, call, or email. The public registration account-matching page exposes phone/enrollment code, SSN4, DOB, first/middle/last name and suffix; DOB, first name, and last name have required asterisks; phone/enrollment code, SSN4, middle name, and suffix do not have asterisks (server validation unverified). Account creation, code receipt, credential entry, and MFA belong to applicant.
4. Review/initial the no-existing-household-benefit certification; choose program-based or income-based route. Prepare current participation proof or household-income proof as applicable. A BenefitsCal application in progress is not enrollment proof.
5. Follow the economic-household decision tree below. Complete tribal/TTY sections only on applicable routes. Identify benefit-qualifying child/dependent if using that person’s participation.
6. Provide applicant legal name, SSN4 (or inspect tribal-ID alternative), DOB, and optionally notification format. Review certification text before signature. Legal guardian/POA signer is expressly permitted with signer bubble; no blanket BenefitsCal-representative substitution.
7. Applicant reviews, signs/dates, and submits required proof with application by actual response date. Paper mailing location is Administrator, P.O.Box138014, Sacramento CA95813; online portal is another route. Signature/initials/submit are human execution boundaries. Research stops before them.
8. Administrator verifies identity and returns approval/denial. Additional identity/household documentation may be requested. No discounts or approval outcome are presumed.

California and federal Lifeline are **separate enrollment/renewal processes** per CPUC September17,2026 announcement. Providers may offer dual enrollment only with disclosures about separate administration/privacy/data sharing and affirmative consent; exact combined form is provider-specific and not inventoried here.

## Economic-household decision tree

| Question | Yes route | No route |
|---|---|---|
| Q1: live with another adult? | Q2 | Skip Q2/Q3; initial B |
| Q2: that adult receives California LifeLine? | Q3 | Skip Q3; initial B |
| Q3: share income and living expenses? | Source states existing same-household benefit problem; do not auto-certify | Multiple households at one address; initial A and B |

Adult includes emancipated minors. Source says an adult with no/minimal income supported by another adult belongs to that economic household. Household boundaries cannot be inferred solely from BenefitsCal residents or buy/prepare-food answers. The separate 4-page worksheet is issued when administrator records show another recipient at the address; p3 repeats the tree, p4 adds participant name, signature/date, and guardian/POA bubble. Standalone worksheet sample is dated December25,2022 and has stronger prosecution wording than incorporated 2026 sample; preserve source-specific certification text.

## Documents and evidence

- **Program-based:** sample pp10/24 asks copy of qualifying-program evidence. Examples: program identification card/number, SSI or other qualifying-program benefits statement, participation/eligibility decision letter, other official documents. Selection includes current Medi-Cal, CalFresh, SSI, Section8/public housing, TribalTANF, TribalHeadStart, BIAGeneralAssistance, FDPIR, veterans/survivor pension, LIHEAP, NSLP, WIC, or common TANF/CalWORKs/StanWORKs/WTW/GAIN bubble.
- **Income-based:** sample pp10/24–25 asks annual household gross income proof. Examples: prior-year tax-return front page, income/pay statements for three consecutive months within previous12 months, SocialSecurity/VA/pension/unemployment/workers-compensation/assistance statements, alimony/child-support documents, other official documents. Do not send originals.
- **Identity:** root agent observed acceptable-ID modal on public registration page. Types: U.S.driver license; citizenship/naturalization certificate; U.S./territory or foreign passport; government/military/state/Tribal ID showing DOB and/or SSN and/or Tribal ID; military ID; Common Access Card only for active/reserve/selected reserve; qualifying military discharge record; Northern Mariana ID; Permanent Resident/Resident Alien/reentry permit; Temporary Resident ID; Employment Authorization; Mexican Consular2006/2014/2015+; California parolee ID within six months of picture date; U.S.visa; California DMV ID. Paper p2 says copy may be submitted; administrator confirms identity. Full validity/upload constraints remain unresolved. JSON includes observed types.
- **Emancipated minor:** p1 explicitly asks emancipation proof.
- **TTY second benefit:** p13/25 asks TTY user name when DDTP supplied TTY; otherwise a copy of medical certificate demonstrating TTY need. General disability facts are insufficient.
- **Tribal:** p13 asks complete tribal ID and initials for Enhanced LifeLine/Link-Up federally recognized tribal-land route. p21 guideline also permits complete tribal ID as alternative to SSN4; exact live routing unresolved.

## BenefitsCal reuse and new collection

Reusable candidates after applicant review: legal name, DOB, verified addresses/contact email/phone, and SSN4 derived from correct person’s SSN. Chosen child/dependent identity can come from corresponding household record. Existing current approval notices, pay statements, tax records, and benefits statements may be reusable evidence if their person/date/period meet LifeLine instructions.

Needs new/confirmed answers: provider/service selection and enrollment code, current program participation rather than historical assistance, LifeLine economic-household boundaries, other adult’s current LifeLine benefit, qualifying-child/dependent choice, annual gross-income aggregation/period, tribal land/ID, TTY-specific route and evidence, format preference, signature/initials, guardian/POA authority, online account/MFA, and optional demographic responses. Never transfer signatures, passwords, or benefit-specific certifications. JSON `reuse_from_benefitscal` states per-field boundaries using Q references to the paper BenefitsCal crosscheck.

## Source conflicts and remaining verification

- No account creation, authentication, signature, submission, real applicant data, or provider contact performed. No authenticated LifeLine form inspected.
- Public sample PDF is paper_reference_not_live, not exact online schema; sample identifiers/addresses/dates are placeholders. A 26-page download supersedes stale indexed 16-page descriptions.
- Public registration Step1 required asterisks observed by root agent; later steps blocked without real provider case. Other public FAQ statements obtained from official search-index extracts; no authenticated UI observed.
- PIN is not a current Customer Portal login field: official portal help says email/password/MFA replaced PIN December15,2023. A provider-specific account PIN or historic mailed PIN is distinct and not cataloged as current requirement.
- Version-specific conflicts: sample p23 >10-person income prose uses $8,500/$100,600 but its own 2026 table and CPUC annual page use $8,700 increment. No threshold calculations provided.
- Sample p21 says one broadband OR phone benefit, while current official FAQ describes eligible bundled phone/broadband. Provider/pilot service configuration needs current confirmation.
- Applicant SSN4 starred required on p15 but p21 permits complete tribal ID alternative; live branch not inspected. Do not impose universal SSN requirement from one label.
- Current ID and eligibility-proof types observed in registration modals by root agent. Request-specific additional-proof forms, complete document-validity rules, upload size/file types, professional/provider APIs, and post-login screen order were not verified.
- LifeLine household is economic unit; BenefitsCal residence or food-preparation household cannot automatically determine it. Pending BenefitsCal application is not proof of qualifying-program participation.
- Required values describe source wording: explicit, conditional, not_marked, unknown. Explicit optional preferences are recorded in condition; not_marked never means mandatory.
- California and federal Lifeline have separate processes per September17,2026 CPUC announcement; this catalog covers California, not federal National Verifier fields.
- No eligibility advice: captured questions, evidence categories, and source boundaries only.
