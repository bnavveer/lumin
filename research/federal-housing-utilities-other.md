# Federal benefits: housing, utilities, other (2026)

Researched 2026-10-11. 2026 FPL = $15,960 for 1 person, +$5,680 per extra person. UNCONFIRMED = not verified against a primary source.

## Section 8 Housing Choice Voucher
- Income ≤50% of area median income (AMI); 75% of new admissions must be ≤30% AMI (statutory). Tenant pays about 30% of adjusted income. Waitlists vary by PHA and are often closed. https://www.hud.gov
- Being on SNAP, Medicaid, SSI or TANF does **not** auto-qualify you; it's an income test only.
- Proposed rule (Mar 2, 2026): PHAs and PBRA owners could adopt work requirements (up to 40 hrs/wk) and/or term limits (≥2 years) for ages 18–61, with exemptions for disabled people, pregnant people, caregivers of kids under 6 and students. Comments closed May 1, 2026. Final status UNCONFIRMED. https://www.nahro.org/news/hud-term-limits-and-work-requirements-proposed-rule-published
- Proposed mixed-status rule (Feb 20, 2026): would bar new families unless every member has eligible status and end prorated aid. Final status UNCONFIRMED. https://www.nahro.org/news/new-2026-hud-mixed-status-proposed-rule-makes-major-changes

## Public housing
- ≤80% AMI, with priority for ≤30% AMI. Example: Toledo, OH 80% limit for 1 person = $53,450. https://toledo.oh.gov/departments/housing-community-development/housing/income-eligibility

## Project-based Section 8, Section 202 (62+), Section 811 (disabled)
- Limits are per property, typically 50–80% AMI. FY2026: Section 202 ≈ $1.031B, Section 811 $287M. https://www.ncsha.org/?p=148329

## LIHEAP
- 150% FPL ($23,940 for 1) or 60% of state median income. Categorical eligibility via SNAP/SSI/TANF at state option. https://liheapch.ncat.org/node/2113
- FY2026 funded at about $4.045B. The FY2027 White House budget proposes elimination (Congress has rejected this before). https://www.publicpower.org/periodical/article/remaining-fy-2026-liheap-funds-disbursed-states-liheap-again-faces-elimination-fy-2027

## Weatherization Assistance Program
- 200% FPL (UNCONFIRMED this session). FY2026 funded. https://www.energy.gov/documents/wap-wpn-26-2pdf

## Lifeline
- 135% FPL: $21,546 (1), $44,550 (4). Up to $9.25/mo (Tribal $34.25). Qualifies via SNAP, Medicaid, SSI, FPHA or VA pension. https://www.lifelinesupport.org
- The Affordable Connectivity Program ended June 1, 2024. https://usac.org/about/affordable-connectivity-program/

## Child care and Head Start
- CCDF: up to 85% of state median income. https://acf.gov/sites/default/files/documents/occ/CCDF-Family-Income-Eligibility-Levels-by-State.pdf
- Head Start: ≤100% FPL; categorical for foster children, homeless children, and TANF/SSI families.

## Education
- Pell Grant 2026–27: max $7,395, min $740. https://fsapartners.ed.gov/knowledge-center/library/dear-colleague-letters/2026-01-30/2026-27-federal-pell-grant-maximum-and-minimum-award-amounts

## Legal aid (LSC)
- 125% FPL: $19,950 (1), $41,250 (4). Effective Jan 26, 2026. https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-01-26/html/2026-01431.htm

## Navigation
- VITA free tax prep: about $67k–$69k income limit (UNCONFIRMED exact). https://irs.treasury.gov/freetaxprep
- 211 for local help. Benefits.gov was retired in Sept 2024; the benefit finder now lives at usa.gov. https://digital.gov/2024/08/07/department-of-labor-general-services-administration-to-centralize-government-benefits-information-on-usagov-and-usagov-en-espanol/
- Unemployment insurance is state-run and not means-tested.
