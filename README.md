# Low-Income Benefits Guide (U.S. + California, 2026)

A one-page interactive guide to what a low-income person can qualify for, like food stamps, Medi-Cal/Medicaid and Section 8, and where to apply for each. It covers federal rules for every state, plus a detailed California section.

Rules and dollar amounts were researched on **October 11, 2026**. Benefits change often and states differ, so confirm with the office that runs each program before relying on a number.

### The page

1. **Income ladder (calculator):** enter household size, yearly income and your situation (working, pregnant, kids, 60+/65+, disabled). Every program's income limit is drawn to scale against the 2026 poverty line, and each row shows **Likely**, **Needs [something]** or **Over limit**. A "Where do you live?" switch changes between California rules and other states. Each row has a **Where to apply** panel.
2. **Where to go in California:** the offices and websites, starting with BenefitsCal, the one application for CalFresh, Medi-Cal and CalWORKs.
3. **Where to apply, benefit by benefit:** 33 benefits in 7 groups (Food, Cash, Health, Housing, Bills and phone, Kids and school, Taxes and legal). Each lists the California route, the other-states route, what to bring and how long it takes.
4. **What California adds on top of federal rules:** for example, CalFresh up to 200% of the poverty line vs. SNAP's 130%, free school meals for every student, and SSI/SSP at $1,233.94/mo vs. $994.
5. **One "yes" opens other doors:** a diagram showing how being on SNAP, Medicaid, SSI or TANF automatically qualifies you for other programs.
6. **Doors by situation** (working, pregnant, kids, 65+/disabled, etc.).
7. **What changed in 2025–2027:** the federal 2025 law (SNAP and Medicaid work rules, immigrant eligibility), California Medi-Cal changes, HUD proposals, and the ACA subsidy expiration.

### Key 2026 numbers

- Federal poverty line: **$15,960** for 1 person, **+$5,680** per extra person (48 states + D.C.)
- SNAP (from Oct 1, 2026): up to **$306/mo** for 1 person, **$1,023** for 4. Gross income limit 130% FPL (CalFresh: 200%)
- Medicaid / Medi-Cal adults: 138% FPL (≈ $22,025 for 1). Medi-Cal kids 266%, pregnancy 213% (MCAP to 322%)
- SSI: $994/mo federal; SSI/SSP in California: $1,233.94/mo
- WIC: 185% FPL ($29,526 for 1)
- Section 8: income at or below 50% of area median income (varies by county)

## Accuracy notes

- Facts marked **UNCONFIRMED** or **(U)** in the research files came from search results or secondary sources, because the agency's own page couldn't be loaded. Check them before relying on them.
- Some numbers are calculated from the poverty line rather than taken from an official table (e.g. the CalFresh limit of ≈ $2,660/mo for 1 person, Medicaid's 138% ≈ $22,025).
- The calculator is a screening guide, not an eligibility decision. Offices count income differently (some subtract rent or child care first). If you're close to a limit, apply anyway.
- Not sure where to start? Call or text **211** for free local help.
